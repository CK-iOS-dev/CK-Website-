Is AI bad for humans?

A new research has been done, in which results have shown that humans are becoming more dependent on Ai more and more, day by day. While, it feels convinient to our daily life, it is making us dumber day by day. We are losing our own ability to think and becoming reliant on AI solely. And of course, that is one of the best case scenario for AI corporations as their applications are being used a lot.

But here is something, every time something new comes to the market, a new invention, is treated like a danger for most of the time. Books, wheels, calculator, computer, iPhone were all treated like dangerous to humanity, and they will destroy us. It is not new with AI, it is just what normally happens.

However, AI is not pure evil. Just like we use other things, we can use AI to upskill ourselves, it really helps. Yet the masses are not going to develop skills from it, they would use it only for their own convinience and not developing any skills. Only a few will develop skills ever. Just like when books were invented, they thought books will be proven dangerous. I won’t say the opposite happened but yeah gaining knowledge didn’t become a mass habit.

I personally use Ai to think better, not replace my own thinking. Because then AI doesn’t serve, it rules you. You lose your agency, and loss of agency to AI, would mean bending to its will. It ay not have biases, but it is not flawless either. So, use Ai and think, just do it carefully.
