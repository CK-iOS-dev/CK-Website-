Lord Mountbatten's role in the independence of India and its unification.

Lord Mountbatten, many know that he was the last viceroy of the British Raj. He came to office to quickly settle the process of the independence of the Indian subcontinent. He was given the date for June 1948, but the process had to be fastened. So the date became 15 August 1947. It all had to be done fast.

There were three parties :

1. INC - Indian Congress party which wanted the entire subcontinent independent and united, fearing the partition of India, they wanted a unified India. They had been in the struggle for long, with some popular figures like MK Gandhi, J. Nehru, and S.Patel.

2) Muslim league - It had emerged later than congress but it demanded a separate state for muslims, fearing that the Indian continent being dominated by hindus, might lead to muslim suppression. They wanted more than they got.

3. Princely States - They were 560 little kingdoms who were in the protection of the British but only till independence, the British legally gave them choice of aligning with either independent dominion, or staying independent.

Mountbatten had to deal with all these three, INC and Muslim League had several disagreement, despite INC insisting unified India, British Raj had to be two independent dominions. While Pakistan demanded quite more, it got limited.

But the problem was both INC and Muslim League, didn’t want so many little kingdoms to exist in their neighbourhood. Mountbatten was asked to negotiate with princely states to join either state. Those princely states had no choice, and forcefully had to join one of India or Pakistan.

Was he important?

Very much so. He was important to make a peaceful transfer of power, while socially things were not peaceful, but politically it was. He was crucial for negotiating with princes, but actually the iron fist of Patel could work, but that would have resulted in a more violent unification.

So while people regard Patel, people forget Mountbatten. He shouldn’t be forgotten, he didn’t play less role than anyone.
