MacBook Air M5

I consider it the Rolls Royce of laptops. It is a damn good device to have. I love it. This is one of the best things for me to have in my life. I like how smooth it is. I like the way it works. I like how it runs. There are a few reasons I bought the MacBook Air M5 and not a typical laptop.

  1. macOS - It is one of the most stable operating system in the desktop world. It doesn’t require me to clean it up every time I open it, just like I used to do on my windows desktops. It is more reliable, because it is heavily controlled. I just open/close the lid, don’t even shut it down everyday, only shutdown once in a week.
  2. Battery Life - It depends heavily on usage, but due to mixed usage, I’m absolutely getting a descent battery life. While when I run heavy tasks, battery is expected to drain. But I can do so, while I’m plugged though I can simply do heavy use for 5 hours easy.
  3. Build Quality - It feels good to even touch this machine, the built is reliable, it looks so good that you keep looking at it repeatedly when closed. It feels premium all the time, no plastic like rubbish. Just minimalist design, no BS.
  4. Camera - It features a 12mp camera, when you hold video calls on it, the quality is descent, not only video calls but the camera is actually capable of much. Centre stage, studio light, edge light, background removing features distinguish it from a typical desktop camera.
  5. Specifications - It features all new M5 processor, far more capable than those of M1 and M2 while significantly ahead of M3 & M4. Offers a 16GB LPDDR5 unified memory, and 512GB as the base storage now.
