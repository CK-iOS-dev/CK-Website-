Price of Perfection.

We have been told that perfection doesn’t exist and that each of the things which is created is flawed and flaws cannot be escaped. We excuse it be like “human creation”. What is perfection then? I think whatever you do with all the potential you could give into it, that is perfect. If that is perfect, what is the price? You won’t understand unless you are not given some examples.

Napoleon - Known for his extra ordinary skills of leading the army and the nation, winning 70 Battles, fighting 7 coalitions and coming out victorious. Building a continental empire, and waging war against every single major power of his time. Becoming the emperor of the French. Doing all this before age 50. That is pure mastery. Extra ordinary by every single standard. How? Driven by ambition, 6 hours of sleep, constant thinking of 100+ ideas how he could be killed in the battle, how could he los. He prepared himself as much as he could, he used his brain to its fullest extent. It wasn’t easy, it was the price he payed for perfection.

Steve Jobs - Known for co founding Apple at his house’s garage. Creating revolutionary products - Lisa, Macintosh, iMac, iPod, MacBooks, iPhone and iPad. From a garage to world’s most valuable company, that’s what he did. Obsessing over design, believing his time is limited and that those who obsess over software should make their own hardware, turning iPhone screens into gorilla glass from plastic within 6 weeks. He was often called rude by his colleagues, often insulting others while praising them the next moment. His price was again obsession.

CK - Known for immense philosophical depth, resilience, pragmatism, at young age. He hasn’t created big projects yet, to be known at a global level but he is onto his path of doing so. He recognises the loop better than Steve, and he has multi view thinking like Napoleon. He has longevity mindset like Bryan Johnson. He is already one of the most successful people, as he understands that success is momentary not absolute.
