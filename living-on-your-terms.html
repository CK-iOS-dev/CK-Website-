Living on your terms.

We have heard about this idea from so many people. But we have heard it at the same time that we shouldn’t misuse the term, to do anything impulsively. Ofcourse, somebody could have terms that consider it okay to break social order, to harm public property, and create unnecessary chaos.

But those are often loose terms, for example, someone expressing them freely could be called a threat to social order because the majority doesn’t like it. Someone could be called a “terrorist” for just living on their terms. What exactly is it?

To live on your terms, is to have more control over your decisions, and live how you want. But you have to deal with a world that wants to operate you. Society wants to program you, so that you obey it. Humans are naturally drawn to validation, that makes them believe whatever others agree upon, is what he should do. Whatever they dislike, should be disliked by him. There is not just society. There is government then, it wants share in everything. It collects taxes, for all the things that you earn, buy, and sell.

How can you live on yours terms in such a world?

I think, that idea becomes impractical. Because the more you try to do what you want, the more you are troubled, it feels like fighting with almost everyone everyday or leave them, get isolated. The idea of isolation scares you as well. That makes the situation tense, there comes a quote about it :

“You were born, not because you wanted. You will die, not because you like it. You still live how you don’t want.”

There is no reason to not trust your heart, your life is mortal, 80-100 years max, you have less to lose and more to gain. Try to live how you want, as much as possible, whenever you get the control.
