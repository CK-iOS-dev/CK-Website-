A Quality Life

We all want to live long lives, 80-100 years. We wanna have big families, earn a lot of money. Live happily. But have we ever thought something alternate?

There were people who changed the world within a few years of age, and we still regard them. There are a few examples, like Steve Jobs. He didn’t just bring new products, he just changed how we interact with technology. Like Michael Jackson, he didn’t just make music, he transformed it. Like Bruce Lee, who wasn’t just a martial artist but he had invented his own. Like Napoleon, who didn’t just become a general, but he made a continental empire.

All these figures died nearly 50 years of age, and Bruce Lee was only 32. But they did what others cannot even do in 100 years of life. They were ambitious from a young age, they wanted to be big always, and they were ready to work for hours and hours like they did. Napoleon only slept 6 hours, Steve Jobs worked for his passion continuously, Michael Jackson couldn’t sleep due to his projects, and Bruce Lee punched 5,000 times a day.

But these were successful figures, in terms of changing the world. Yet their life couldn’t be termed as a quality life because they didn’t enjoy it. How can you? When your work and life balance is ruined. When you are dedicating several hours a day over and over.

Steve Jobs could be remembered for Macintosh 1984 alone. He didn’t have to create more products. Michael could stop at “Thriller”, he didn’t need to do anything more. Bruce could only act in a few films then command good rates for his martial arts lessons like he did, he didn’t have to do more. Napoleon needn’t become an emperor, he was a great general already.

At the same time, we see those who retired early after a bit of success, and they live good lives. They don’t have to be stressed. They don’t have to follow the loop those so called successful guys were stuck into. Yes, they were trapped in the loop. And those who have been satisfied by some success and are enjoying now, aren’t trapped at all.

A life that is of quality, is what you live on your own terms. Not on the terms of others. And even those loop stuck people whom we call successful, did live on their terms, that’s why their life was worth to them. Not because they change the world for us.

LIVE ON YOUR TERMS.
