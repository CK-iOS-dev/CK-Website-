The fight of two bulls and the condition of the grass.

It is a common scenario where two bulls are fighting with each other, but whoever wins, the grass will get punished. I mean, a lot of grass is suppressed by those two bulls, and whoever wins, grass won’t remain safe.

I want to explain the so called cosmic phenomenon by writing this. I’m saying that whether the white bull (Considerably good) wins or the black bull (Considerably bad) wins, the grass (people) will suffer in both the cases. Let’s say what we live in today, is a world ruled by ‘white bull’, I mean “God” of any kind here, do you see that the world has minimal suffering? No, in fact, the world is as imperfect as much it would be under a demon, but he’d only prefer the sustainability of this planet, not fairness. “God” acts like such a demon, if it exists. While total destruction won’t happen straight away but everyday life won’t be lesser than destruction.

What is that? We assume that this world is being ruled by a good, knowledgeable, and powerful being. Yet we don’t see such a perfection in his creation. If “God” exists then why are we living like that “God” is irrelevant? If we have to do it all by ourselves, why pray? If a demon would want devotion from others, he’d be called arrogant, but when “God” demands constantly wanting praise, submission, and devotion, nobody calls him arrogant. Are we believing “truth” and “good” are different from what “God” does, or whatever he does and says is good and truthful? From reading every kind of scripture, you’d see the answer is that “God” is the source of good, not the other way around.

Whoever wins, your life will be screwed unless you’re born rich or you are working on yourself, no bull would solve your issue. None of the bull is good. Both are bad, yet victory could be one bull’s.
