Blessing, Curse, and Absurdity.

We have been told that there are some good things that happen to us, those are blessings. There are bad things that happen to us, those are curses. But reality is way more complex than what we are told. What we know is whatever the evidence backs, and what we have seen, and what is logically compelling, We find it true. We find it reality. Does Evidence support it? Let’s see.

Let’s define these words :

Blessing = Blessed by someone

Curse = Cursed by someone

Absurdity = Something weird

Evidence doesn’t support that if someone wishes something, and says something about someone, then it would come true. Maybe, it might come true by coincidence, but it ain’t certain, it is rare, but not so rare, but still quite so.

Have we seen anything like that happen? Yeah, sometimes what we say becomes the truth sometimes, what we wish, comes to reality, but those are again coincidental ones, not something that we can trust most of the time.

Is it even logically compelling? Logically, we shouldn’t be believing in something like this idea, as by wishing or cursing, happens nothing, it creates no results in reality, however, psychologically that is different.

Psychologically, we can use cursing to calm down our anger. Especially, if done privately, it creates no bad impact. It rather helps you to get rid of the anger, but I must say you must rather avoid doing that publicly, as that possibly can have some negative impact, and that can later harm.

If we wish “Good” for others, it gives us mental peace, we think that we feel the “Goodness” ourselves, and that might be considered valuable by some.

The absurdity is that your curse or your blessing, have no actual worth, yet what we think is that it might create some impact. Actually, sometimes it can happen, but mostly it doesn’t. And what happens like that is coincidence, not a miracle.

My view is that, we can use these “tools” but we should acknowledge that these have no real world worth, and we should therefore think practically about it. While many may find these tools as valuable, it weighs less on my criterions. As logically, it doesn’t make sense, evidence doesn’t support it, and the things are absurd. The way we feel about something, doesn’t mean our feeling is righteous.
