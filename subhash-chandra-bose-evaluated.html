Subhash Chandra Bose evaluated like never before...

Subhash Chandra Bose, known as Netaji throughout India, for his efforts in the independence movement of India. His most famous quote “Give me blood, I will give you freedom” is recognised by several people across the Indian subcontinent. He made Indian National Army (INA) with the help of the Japanese Empire, he created his own currency, flag for India, and he was a communist person. He is considered to have died in a plane crash, while some believe that he was alive as Gumnami Baba for his later years.

This is typical information, and it is not an evaluation or analysis at all. This is barely information. As much as you would notice is narrative of him being a hero. And that is all we are taught, aren’t we?

Well, let’s critique the narrative and make the truth appear :

Objection 1 : “Netaji” is an exaggeration. Anyone could be a “Neta” (Politician), nothing special. The problem is that he was not a politician at all, because a politician doesn’t create a coup. He did have a party, but for what? He believed that independence won’t arrive peacefully but it could be taken through strength, which later proved to be wrong. As his army couldn’t win over the colonial regime.

Objection 2 : “Give me blood, I will give you freedom” he said, but that is very easy to say when you are a leader. Those who will die are the followers. If the path was effective, Bose must have given his blood first before recommending it to others. If blood is given, then freedom for whom? He literally meant that one should give up life for freedom, if one does so, one still doesn’t get any freedom. If partial blood giving was allowed then still a person who has no energy, what does freedom mean?

Objection 3 : He created INA, at first he went to Nazis, Hitler specifically, who himself believed India must not be independent. Hitler sent him to Japan. Japanese Empire, itself wanted to capture India from the British. Still, it agreed to give Bose prisoners of war captured in Singapore, weapons and funding. Honestly, those two were greater enemies, more horrific, and quite racist. He met with the country’s enemies.

Objection 4 : He was a communist, his ideology and what he was trying to do, indicates he may have wanted to rule India personally. I am not certain but that possibility is too strong, especially when he insisted others to give blood for freedom. I believe, it is good that India had a transfer of power. Not a military conflict for independence. Because then a dictator was obvious.

Objection 5 : He may have died in a plane crash, it is a mainstream theory, even if we say he switched to Gumnami Baba, it is not a good news as well, because he had lost, he had to hide and since he could no longer come out as Subhash, Subhash was died either way. It doesn’t matter whether he was alive afterwards, he couldn’t even come back even if we believe Gumnami Baba theory.
