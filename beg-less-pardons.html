Beg less pardons!!

We are living in a world that pretends to be polite, but at the same time it exploits the polite one. The nice guy, who wants to hurt nobody’s feelings. The guy who always keeps softening his words, so that nobody hates him. He wants to be liked by everyone. He cannot tolerate criticism, he doesn’t handle insult, and feels demotivated. He feels guilty, for even little errors.

Saying “Sorry” or simple apologising has become a very noticeable thing in the society, but I have observed that often the society is not saying what it is saying. Some people keep apologising, because they feel guilty due to their moral programming. In a few nations like Great Britain, people say “sorry” even when they don’t even have to.

I’ve got reminded of a quote on this : If the son is wise, why save money? If the son is foolish, why save money?

It just means in this context that if your mistake is little, don’t worry, but if it is big, no “sorry” will protect you. A real life example, suppose if two people crashed into each other, nothing big has happened. But if one hurts another willingly, then no pardon will save him.

Yes, it is not necessarily binary like big mistake vs small, sometimes the mistake is neither, but can be settled by “Sorry”, and it does make sense then.

What I am saying is, One must rather improve than apologise. An apology gives you a chance though, but how long if you don’t work on it?

Work upon your flaws, and stop apologising for little things, unless you have not crossed a legal boundary, nobody can do anything about you. Except violence, but still there is a law for handling it, and it is better handled legally. The court can’t punish the one who has been hurt or has only defended when it became necessary.
