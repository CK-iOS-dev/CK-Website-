Ignorance or Awareness?

You’d be thinking why have I put these two opposite words, and or in them. You’d think everyone knows that Ignorance is not the way to go. Awareness is what everyone wants. However, I don’t find it true. People interpret it differently. This doesn’t mean they have actually reached what they are claiming to have reached.

Ignorance. People claiming to have “Awareness”, are usually claiming spiritual awareness, metaphysical BS. They are ignorant. Those who are drinking whiskey or wine everyday and running away from the fact it is injurious to health. They break rules while knowing that they shouldn’t. Because they are habitual to it.

Someone will argue that they know the consequences, but they are still approaching. But that isn’t like smoking a cigratte and admitting after that they know their life will be reduced and they are okay with that, because they think that living more will not add much value, but they are accepting the harsh cost of it. They will not only die early, but at the same time suffer in the later days.

What is Awareness then?

It is about taking a decision that has risks but still those must be calculated, like you can become an airforce pilot but you are aware of the fact that you can crash, and it can even lead to death. But still that tasks is safer than most assume. One must be ready to attempt it despite risk, if it is actually what they want to pursue and are okay with a bit of risks.
