Those who hunger strike

There are situations in this world where the governments don’t hold accountability, for the wrong doings, for the scandals, for the frauds that happen. The nation is harmed, yet the intensity is dealt with a simple apology, a simple statement despite knowing that the time for statements is gone, the time has come when action is required, not a mere set of words. But instead, an absurd silence is observed. No response, the issue suppressed in a matter of days, and again the belief “Everything is okay now”.

If a scandal happens, if a fraud happens, usually the democratic governments of this world deal with it by suppressing the issue with some other noise. If, in case, the voice is raised, then as much as an investigation would be held, finding some of the accused ones guilty and perhaps leaving those with bigger names. But that is not it, the root cause stands still, the roots will produce those errors over and over again. To deal with the root cause, a reform is essential, so that the risk is minimised significantly. To seek a reform, one has to protest heavily, though peaceful entirely.

But protests don’t lead to reforms alone, in many cases. A known figure has to be on a hunger strike, for days and days. Perhaps, it warns the governments that it is the time top finally act. However a few times, the governments are very disinterested about such things, they don’t even pay a little attention, to** those who hunger strike. **But I am pretty sure about it, even if a so called reform happens in any case, there always remains a reason to hunger strike, to protest, and the achievement is not so bigger than the tip of a bigger iceberg.

This ain’t about one government, one country, one man, one movement. It is broader than all of it, it happens in many nations, I don’t think it is going to stop ever, because the confusion remains and confusion doesn’t let things happen.
