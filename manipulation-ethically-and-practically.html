Manipulation, ethically and practically.

Manipulation has been considered an evil tactic by some people, they think it is not good for a person to manipulate other, as it often involves deception, not telling the truth, and tricking the other into a trap. But let us rethink about it in a way that we have never thought about it. Then, it remains a question what manipulation ethically is? and what is it practically?

Is Manipulation ethical? To answer this question ask yourself have you lied to someone? If answer honestly, we all have lied to someone and at sometime, this is quite closer to manipulation. But that is what we don’t usually realise. If we hold on to the assumption that manipulation is unethical, then lying sure is unethical.

Manipulation is immoral and unethical, by almost every single moral and ethical standard, as it requires deception, tricking, and lying. Absolutely, if it was ethical, it would simply be convincing or persuading. However, the line is too blur for you to easily notice which one is which.

Manipulation from practical lens : Manipulation is all about strategy, it involves exactly what we say “Persuading anyhow, at any cost” it just means you’ll convince the other person by hook or by crook, you don’t care about the means, you care about the ends, and as some say “Ends justify the means”, however I don’t think so. I still think, means justify the ends.

My own view : Manipulation is a great tool for strategy, it really is. But it doesn’t mean you have bro risks at all, once caught, you’ll be harshly punished by the society. Ethically, it is for sure, unethical. I neither encourage nor I discourage it.
