The word "Perfect" is a lie...

“Perfect” is an imperfect word itself, it is pure imagination away from reality. How can it be even achieved? People talk about things ask if there is something perfect. Perfection cannot be achieved. People still obsess over perfection. They think that they can create an absolute product.

Yet we cannot say the concept is only useless, because if we justify everything imperfect and flawed by saying “It couldn’t be perfect anyway”. That is anti growth. Still people would use this loophole to avoid improvement like they do. But improving al the time also creates a loop. “Perfect” is a word people can work as hard as they can to create the best product they are able to.

So what should we d at all? What is the truth?

Actually, “Perfect” is a lie. You cannot achieve “Perfection” because that doesn’t exist. It is only a lie until you don’t prove it true. Therefore, it doesn’t justify when one says “Well, everything is flawed” or “Nothing can ever be perfect”, when one works hard, builds up something beautiful, and put all the effort you could.

Only then you can achieve perfection, still it doesn’t mean there’s already something what we can call “Perfect”. Those who are comfortable with making excuses can say “No, perfection exists, therefore even my flawed creation is perfect”. While those who accept the uncomfortable path of achieving “Perfection”, reach a point where they can say “Perfect is a lie but I’ll make it come true”.
