How did they? (The mind of ambitious people)

We all imagine living normal lives, but we wish to live a luxury, rich, and affluent life. We don’t have any business plans to get there, and we settle for normal stuff. We normalise with the situation, and don’t think above a certain threshold. We admit the limits, and try to live like everyone else does.

The Death of Dreams : We dream big once in a while in our lives, we are children at that time, we think we can achieve it. What happens is that we grow up and our dreams start dying, we get rid of what we once thought to be. We think that we can’t achieve what we thought we would. And we call it “maturity”.

To be stuck with your dreams and achieve them, you’ve got to be very serious about them. And most people can’t jump from there. They dream, and their dreams keep changing day by day. They don’t have a clear goal and don’t work towards their goals, and they excuse it anyhow. Yes, constraints are real. But you’ll only win once you get over them and not let them limit you.

An ambitious man like Napoleon, John D Rockefeller, and Richard Branson, etc have these traits common ;

  1. Self Belief - They strongly believed they will become what they want to be.
  2. Strategic Mind - They didn’t have belief only, they corrected their mistakes to actually make their belief true.
  3. Big Ambition - They didn’t settle for typical lifestyle, they thought of top always.
  4. Logic - They used logic, but not absolutely, because intuition has to be trusted too.
