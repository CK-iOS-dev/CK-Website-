Trauma of a Child

We all have been in our childhood, there is no doubt!! 😅 but today I wanna raise an issue that we barely think about, and rarely talk about it. I simply mean about the childhood traumas. All of us have experienced traumas, little or big. Today it might seem “Oh, it was not too big” but then it was really big. Just think about the mindset you had at that time. But today, we are not just talking about subjective experiences, in fact, I would rather focus on experiences that are common to all of us.

Common things you have to do as a child :

Respect the elders - They program you the way they were programmed, they want obedience, yeah they are still not selfish 😂, they are still kind. If you ask them why you should obey or respect them? Their answer is whatever but it always means “Because we are elders, that’s why.” But what if you still don’t? Then happens no.2 written below.

Punishment - Whether physical or mental, humiliation is the tactic they use if you don’t obey/respect them, they use both weapons without even realising what could even the impact be, and of course, the impact is not positive at all, it either produces unconfident or like minded children. Fun fact is their parents were also unconfident.

Long term expectations - Whether or not, children are expected to do something in the future. In many poor nations, they become the pensions. They become old age care. Instead of expecting it from the state, they expect it from their children. They want them to be successful in the social sense, but actually you cannot do that. There is always a level to reach, the level that isn’t reached is where one believes he is enough as he is.

This all happens because there exists a hierarchy :

1. Affluent people

2. Well educated people

3. Typical People

4. Children

While affluent children are still children, I consider them in a powerful category​, I want this hierarchy to break. It will still break, once the children get well educated they break it automatically. Therefore, the newer generations must focus on education.
