Do alcohol and smoking really decrease life?

There are various examples of drinkers and smokers who reached their 80s and 90s, but we were told that drinking and smoking cuts the life by a decade or two. It causes several diseases like cancer, liver, kidney diseases. But we see many people who didn’t have any of these diseases despite too much alcohol or smoking several cigars a day. Let me bust a myth today with one of those examples.

Winston Churchill, born in 1874, He was the greatest British prime minister (1940-45, 1951-55). He drank champagne every day, and in significant amounts. He smoked several cigars a day. He handled the world’s most responsible job of his time. Despite all that, he lived up to 90 years, and died in 1965.

But there was a disciplined man, the field Marshall - Bernard Montgomery, Churchill offers him a cigar. He denies, saying “I neither drink nor smoke, and I’m 100% fit”. This mocked Churchill. Churchill replies “I both drink and smoke, I am 200% fit”. In the end, Bernard Montgomery died at 87.

What this tells us? Despite following strict routines, Monty lived 3 years less than Churchill. But Churchill lived this much because of his special genes, which created a buffer for his larger than life sort of lifestyle. Churchill could have lived a few more decades with more quality in his life.

Both men lived almost same years of life, Winston Churchill experienced several strokes in his last years and couldn’t walk much or stand more than few minutes. Bernard Montgomery remained quite active until his last years, even his death happened in sleep, he probably didn’t experience much pain at his death bed compared to Churchill.

Should you go for alcohol or smoking? I advise, for living a quality life even at the last years, don’t consume those things, but if you still do, do it with all your awareness of the trade offs. I am not here to tell what should everyone follow, you decide.
