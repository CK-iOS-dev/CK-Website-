Why is it necessary to be rich?

In the modern era, or even before, somebody, in some way, has wanted to be a rich person. Often it is a debated number, at what one can call themselves “rich”. For an individual, it is different than it is for a family. Still saying that only when one exceeds X amount of money, they enter rich society only then. However, that is subjective and debatable as I’ve already described.

People also have different criterions estimating net worths. Some don’t deduct liabilities out of assets, while others do it in advance for the estimation. Most people are okay with having paper wealth, what that means is, you cannot use it for yourself immediately or even lately. If you have $1,000,000 in equity of a corporation, you are a millionaire. It is a separate question whether it would be sold or be valued at the same amount if sold, even after being sold, taxes are knocking the door. The donation which is forced.

Being a rich person isn’t an easy job either, it requires a lot of effort, whether physical or mental. Sacrifices of health, time, and life itself. We think we are escaping the rat race, but I think that race is a creation of mind, if you strip away competition, you are not a rat. But these millionaires, billionaires, and wealthy groups are themselves in rat race.

Those who think they can sit home and do nothing at all, because they have enough money to support their life, they still would do some sort pop activity. There would still be a goal. There would still be something new, you are trying to achieve. The race doesn’t stop.

My advice to you is, to enjoy the present, and still save a certain amount that secures future to some extent, you don’t have to burn out for a dream that may not be so meaningful once achieved, the race doesn’t end, even for the ones you think, it ends. It doesn’t.
