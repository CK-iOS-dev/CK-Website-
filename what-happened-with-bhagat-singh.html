What happened with Bhagat Singh?

Bhagat Singh - Shahid-Azam, is referred to as India’s one of the most popular rebels, people remember how he was hanged till death at just 23, he was a true nationalist, he had not only wanted to eradicate colonial government but he also wanted to eradicate poverty in India. He believed that freedom couldn’t be achieved peacefully, but it would require a militant struggle.

People believe that the reason behind giving death penalty to Bhagat Singh, was the fear of his influence, and the rising nationalism. Well, I think that was just a factor, not the whole story. Of course, he did have influence. But we have to look at the laws of that time. Those laws will give us a broader view than “He opposed the British, so they hanged him, the British are the culprit”.

According to the English law at that time, if a person murders another, they will be punished. The Capital punishment upon murder was the law of the time, even in England. So obviously, it was so in India. In fact, capital punishment continued to exist in the British judicial system until 1965. But Bhagat Singh’s year was 1931.

Bhagat Singh and his fellows had murdered a policeman, so they were likely gonna be punished. Though they were jailed before, for bsectiombing the central assembly. They were not executed at that time, because the bombs were to make a loud sound. If it had been really harmful, then they would have been executed at that time.

Why did Bhagat Singh kill a policeman? In retaliation to what happened with Lala Lajpat Rai. He had been protesting peacefully, but was beaten severely. The British used section 144 to act upon, like that.

I am not sure why was section 144 necessary at that time. But if you think it’s controversial, I agree. I am not denying that it was a potential exploitation of this law, but has it not happened post independence?

But if a person takes retaliation then? There have been hundreds of Lala Lajpats since. Would you consider it just if you think Bhagat Singh was just?
