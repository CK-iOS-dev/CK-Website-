Realism vs Honesty

A realist often uses the option that gives the best result, but it doesn’t mean that the means that lead to the end become justified just because the end may be good. An honest person might not use unfair means in the first place because they know they might not hide it. If they still approach, they admit afterwards.

A realist may make promise to break it. For making profit or strategy. An honest person holds onto his words, he cannot escape by simply saying “I’ve evolved”. That increases one’s responsibility towards their speech. Whereas, a realist might just avoid the conversation entirely, where his past promises will be recalled and he’d be called a hypocrite.

That doesn’t mean that an honest person cannot be strategic, but they have to delay honesty for that. The more you delay, the more you’re away. Realism is seeing reality as it is, and acting like whatsoever improves your life. An honest person has to be bounded by principles. Honesty is not the path of reward, you must not assume so. Realism is being opportunist, that has material reward.

Why am I calling integrity as honesty? I don’t see them separately. Honesty is being honest to what you said, and acting upon it. Likewise, a realist sees the reality. So can an honest person. But the realist has to act upon whatsoever maximises the survival and life. Honest person can still hold onto principles.

If a realist sees all facts, and doesn’t act upon them, what is real in his view? Mere watching. If an honest person sees reality, and so they can, and choose to act by principles, is because they are honest to themselves. A realist cannot afford to act otherwise, that would end the distinction between a realist and a honest person.
