"Fearless dies once, Cautious never lives"

“Fearless dies once, Cautious never lives”, But it should be fearful never lives. Because a cautious person would follow precautions but will act. Cautious never lives, so overspeed and don’t pay attention. That wouldn’t make you wiser, it would make you stupid. Cautions are important, and one should not ignore them. Because cautions don’t mean inaction, they mean acting responsibly.

Cautions are not the problems, in fact they bring awareness of those which are. Cautions are the beginning, not the end. First step, not last. Cautions don’t stop you, you stop at cautions and blame them. Before we play a game, drive a vehicle, do something risky, cautions don’t stop us. They tell us, Be careful. Things can be risky, but risk with cautions is minimised. It is not the highest level of risk. In fact, cautions make it safer to live.

Cautions are about clarity, but carelessness is about ignorance. To make sure what we are doing is courageous, we should at least have clarity about it. With ignorance, even careless actions could be termed “courage”. With clarity, one knows what they’re doing. Courage after clarity is actually courage. Courage without clarity is ignorance.

To actually “live”, you can compromise with neither quality nor quantity. Yes, one should have decent experiences but with a life lived for longer time could have more such experiences. Caution = Clarity, Cautions first and then courage. While a principle difficult to impose, definitely worth the effort.
