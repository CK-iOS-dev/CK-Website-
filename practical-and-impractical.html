Practical and Impractical.

We have heard both of the words, we are not unfamiliar with them. In fact, those are the words used in our day to day life. It seems like we understand them, but I don’t thin we do. Because as intuitive they seem, they are not. They are intellectually heavy words to be understood, once understood, it brings more clarity into our life. When we become good at distinguishing between Practical and Impractical.

Practical? Let me define it - What helps in survival, increases leverage, makes life comfortable in a meaningful way, doesn’t lead to self destruction. For example, abiding the laws is a practical choice unless very extreme, cause it keeps you safe. You don’t get into trouble. Eating meat is a practical choice, to this very day, because otherwise you begin to lack several nutrients, protein, B12 etc. Working is a practical choice, if you don’t work, you cannot spend.

Impractical? Let me define it - It is what you are ding because it provides satisfaction to your heart to an extent, you feel different, you feel wiser sometimes. Like following principles, consistently over and over, even if they lead to loss, even if they hurt your feelings, even if they make your life uncomfortable. You follow the virtue, even if it pains. Because that provides a certain satisfaction, you won’t have gotten otherwise from material things. It is not only impractical due to its virtuous nature, art is impractical if it leads to constant discomfort, anxiety, stress, and isolation.

I am not here to argue what you should choose in your life, rather I am categorising which is what, so that you can decide it by your own. If comfort, safety, and a long life matters to you, be practical. If you find satisfaction in virtues, be impractical, it is not wrong either. You can follow a hybrid as well, but hybrids don’t fit either category. Like iPads, they aren’t full in the sense Mac and iPhone are. But only when they are compared, otherwise they have their own functionality. Hybrid of practical and impractical can also work.
