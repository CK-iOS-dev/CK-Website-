Is there an absolute truth?

We all have believed at some point of our life that there might be an absolute truth to discover or as if our life is a question that we need to answer. Life is a mystery to be solved. Or Perhaps we have believed that a certain thing is the absolute truth or the ultimate meaning of our life. We often believe such things and rarely enquire them at all.

People can say even claiming that there is no absolute truth becomes an absolute claim itself which I think it really is. So what can we do about this? If we say, 2+2 = 4, that is true in every single case. Still it is not an absolute truth, no matter if the outcome is consistent. And it specifically is a mathematical truth.

Also, we look at laws of physics, you cannot refute it that you will fall down if you jump. The laws of physics cannot be defied , no matter who you are and what you do. Still, not absolute. They are specifically part of physics. If we look at other sciences, such as chemistry, if a certain chemical has certain properties, they remain identical regardless of person. If we look into biology, we still find things that can be called truth. Such truths are scientific not absolute.

Yet there’s something you might be thinking :Those truths are consistent, that’s why they’re truths. Why I’m avoiding the label of absolute truth? It is because while scientific and mathematical truths are real but they don’t give us a meaning. Meaning that helps us live our lives. And I must admit that has to be created. Absolute truths don’t exist, they have to be made 🤫.
