Are Polymaths real?

Polymaths are those who are excellent at more than one skill, considered rare and extraordinary. There are a few famous examples :

1. Leonardo Da Vinci

2. Benjamin Franklin

3. Winston Churchill

But are there actually Polymaths? I would say “No. Not in a way we have been told”.

While a polymath excels at more than one skill, they cannot be compared to those who do that skill specifically. Mastery requires repetition and improvement over time. A person cannot master all at once. Those which we can polymaths had some skills common mostly :

1. Art/Science

2. Statesmanship

3. Writing

Those skills are very common among polymaths. Yet I would say they did those things like a hobby for several years before they became competent in it. I think a modern person has even more chances to become a polymath by that standards. What my position is, Polymathy is real to some extent but an all rounder is not good at something specifically, or is good at one and average at others.

Polymathy is not a special trait, it can be developed over time. Let us say you draw for an hour, write for half, and produce music and the next half. You’re touching three skills in merely 2 hours, which almost everyone these days wastes upon scrolling. After 5 years or 10 years, those skills might not make you a millionaire but yeah you won’t have to go pay check to pay check. You can build multiple income streams by doing so over the years, even if the income only is about living.
