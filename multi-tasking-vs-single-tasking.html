Multi Tasking vs Single Tasking

In this modern world, agree or not we all do multi tasking. But today I am going to reflect at it to figure out what is better for focus, time management, what is good for mental well being, we are not here to follow the trend, we are here to set a tradition. A trend might last a few years, tradition beats it always.

Multi-Tasking - Getting multiple tasks done simultaneously, often switching from one task to another, while acknowledging that the time taken remains exactly what it would take otherwise, because each task will take its own time. You cannot get 4 hours of work done in 1 hour if you work at 4 tasks simultaneously.

Single Tasking - Focusing on one task at a time, because you know that every task will take its time, by juggling from one task to another makes no sense if you cannot get them done fast. Single Task means you are entirely focused on one thing, you don’t use much ram, you don’t heat your computer up.

Focus wise? Single Tasking wins.

Time management? Both will take equal time.

Mental well being? This is a hard question to answer, on one hand, single tasking appears rigid and boring. You are stuck at one task for hours. Multi Tasking ensures you keep switching and don’t get bored. On the other hand, multi tasking itself is not without a flaw, I mean juggling from one task to another.

Traditionally, we did one task at a time, that was all we could do.

Trend, this is about multi tasking. People unnecessarily multi task.

My tradition - I think, if we don’t multi task ever, we never utilise our computer’s power. But multi tasking doesn’t mean juggling. It should mean more convenience. I do single tasking, when there is need of intense power and focus. And I believe you should do the same.
