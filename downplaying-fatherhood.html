Downplaying Fatherhood...

We have seen how much people idealise motherhood, the role of mother in the life of a child, they say a child’s first friend is his/her mother. The life of a child starts from a mother, the mother breastfeeds him/her, the mother teaches him/her to walk and talk. Mother is always with the child, she encourages and nourishes the children.

What about Fatherhood btw? Well, fathers are depicted like unknown people in many traditions. By unknown I mean, secondary, complimentary etc. They are depicted strict and unkind. They are rarely portrayed as caring and comforting. Often we see them shouting and nagging.

The truth is, fathers are equally responsible for their children. They can’t breastfeed, they didn’t give you birth, they weren’t always there. But they led to the very beginning, they provided the seed of which the big tree (you) have grown. Without the sperms, eggs aren’t enough. They can’t be there all the time because they work hard enough, so your mother can. They are responsible for giving you a good life, not a life merely. That role is equally important.

I’m not saying that cultures always depict fathers as unkind, but we can notice that the society we live in, fatherhood is downplayed. A father cannot take the custody of younger children incase of a divorce, while both parents are equal. Many people use the word “mother” regarding the nation etc. Why not father? Why in some countries people don’t even know the word exists.

I’m not saying we should downplay motherhood instead, but we should rather make a better view for fatherhood in front of the world. Fathers can be toxic, so could be mothers. This 21 Jun, give fathers some credit.
