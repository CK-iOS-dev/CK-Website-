Do Evil doers always get punished? Well...

We have been told that evil doers are punished either one way or the other, those who have committed evil, justice will happen and they will rot in jail or get persecuted for their crimes. We are given famous examples where evil doers were life imprisoned or shot down or sentenced to death. And I must say those examples are actually important, to highlight the importance of law and justice. Those examples keep the society peaceful, to an extent.

However, it is not as simple as that. Not all criminals are punished, and not all are punished equally. Yes, I know that is primarily because of the degree of their crimes, punishments couldn’t be equal. But it is beyond that.

A criminal like Pablo Escobar kills several hundred people, but he is not shot hundreds of times. Like, the negative impact he produced, was far larger than what happened to him after all that. How is it justice? This is the maximum you could do. The hundreds of lives lost, would not come back. Yet people can somehow satisfy themselves as he was at least punished to a point.
