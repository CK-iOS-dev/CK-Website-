Churchill Reality.

It is said that Churchill killed 3 million people, so they say. But I as an Indian want to balance the situation and debunk overstated things.

Firstly, it is not possible for a man to kill millions of people. Some context : World War 2 (the most dangerous war world has ever seen) taking a risk by sending food like that when victory wasn’t clear, could mean something else. The officials in bengal often underreported the situation, Churchill didn’t have accurate information. Japan had invaded Burma, and Bengal was near. Japanese invasion destroyed a lot of food too. Not only these, but cyclones ad natural disasters worsened the situation. When the situation had become very visible, the British Indian Army was sent to Bengal and along with significant agriculture in 1944, the famine recovered. Churchill didn’t deny help, he helped partially or debated. Food wasn’t denied right away. So, I think the situation was complex where constraints were real and military units had to be prioritised because without victory there’s no survival. Churchill was no saint, but he was against of fake ones like Gandhi. 3 million is their favourite number while it could be anywhere from 800,000 to 4 million.

Churchill primarily operated under constraints that shaped the policy. Churchill wasn’t hero, he was not a villain. Deaths were real, situation wasn’t black & white.
