The art of giving up

If you have been in a feeling like this : I am struggling with it, I find myself bound to some obligations that I created. I can’t be free, I can’t give up. This feeling comes because of what we have been told over the years, we are told that we should not give up, and giving up is cowardice and sets a wrong precedent.

I think that mindset is wrong. Whether it is giving up or beginning, neither is inherently wrong nor is inherently right. It depends when you do that, but I will talk about specifically when to give up. Giving up is fine, if you have tried over and over. Giving up is fine if you are fed up, your way of thinking has changed. Giving up something doesn’t mean a defeat, but rather letting go of it.

If giving up is bad, then Steve Jobs quitting studies from Reed college must be bad. Actually, if one doesn’t replace it with anything then it becomes a problem, he replaced it with calligraphy and Apple. The key is to divert that time to something you find more meaningful, something that interests you more.

Give up!! and start new. Nothing in it is wrong. A person is not obliged to stay the same for their whole life, because they have to become bigger and bigger over time, the people whom you think are the winners, are winners externally whether they find their so called “Victory” actually feels satisfying to them is a separate unanswerable question, because to somebody Cristiano Ronaldo can feel successful and disciplined, to others he can seem like a hamster on the wheel.
