Don't Die for a principle.

We have often heard about the ideal examples, “He was man of his words” “He is a very honest person” “He always speaks truth” “He always does the right thing” “He is always right”. Those ideals look good on paper, but in reality they require immense sacrifice. You pay a very expensive price for your principles, most people cannot bear that much price, few can, and only a few do.

What does expensive price (immense sacrifice) mean about you?

You get nothing material, your bank account doesn’t get anything, it is not always necessary for others to admire. Your traits and call you a hero, your health might not become better. In fact, the opposite can happen - plausibly. You lose money, you are condemned, and you are stressed. Sometimes, good things can be more. Other times, bad are more.

But if we strip away material and physical condition, we find out that others are too weak at holding onto their words. Only the rich can afford to pay expensive price, cheap people are hypocrite. Those hypocrites might have a few more zeros in bank accounts than us, but they are still cheap. Why they are cheap? They change faster than the weather. A few moments are enough to change them. It gives you a special feeling that you are paying whatever price for it but you’ve got the satisfaction that others don’t. You are a man of principles, ain’t that enough?

Honestly, I agree that principles must have a strong place in our life. If we keep changing and don’t hold onto the very words we said, how could we be trusted? Not at all. That is why, I think, principles are necessary. But my appeal is that one must still not die for them because who’d then stand by those?

You can create any principles, but you have to be consistent, you have to borrow nothing. But exhaustion is not what you should pay for them, and suffering must be limited for them, not unlimited.

“Oh, I am rich, I am a man of principles” is a psychological way of thinking. Not realistic, I think. I still don’t hold onto this assumption tightly despite holding onto my principles.
