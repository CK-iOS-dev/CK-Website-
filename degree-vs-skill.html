Degree vs skill?

We live in a world where the job market is observing a noticeable shift. We keep getting news that thousands of employees are wiped out immediately. Thousands of families are thrown to poverty. While they spent thousands of dollars and so much of time and effort to earn an academic degree.

We are taught obedience, it is observed rarely where employees protest for being wiped out without any fault. And we see it rarely, any corporation employs the people backwards once they are fired. Jobs aren’t easy too. Jobs are becoming increasingly stressful due to rising competition and what was once a job of work-life balance, becomes a cage of ruined lifestyle.

Salaries are not enough, working class often applies loan to meet social standards. Those loans don’t elevate the standards but they alleviate them. Rising debts lead to more dependence on jobs.

Degree or skill?

Skills are becoming more and more relevant. It is without a doubt clear that people have always been like skipping conventional path and got succeeded. This is no joke.

What I wanna say is : Non conventional paths coffer success too.
