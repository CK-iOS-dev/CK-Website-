Western vs Eastern philosophy, which is superior?

The debate is very old, whether Western philosophy is superior or is Eastern superior. Eastern ones say they are superior while Western ones say they are. But reality is a bit nuanced.

Major Western Philosophies : Existentialism, Liberalism, Utilitarianism, Real Politik, etc. The focus primarily is the individual, usually no higher claim like salvation etc. No higher entity. While metaphors exist, they are metaphors explicitly. Purely based on real life, often challenging dominant religions, and trying to fill the void of nihilism.

Major Eastern Philosophies : Hindu thought, Buddhism, Jainism, Taoism, Shintoism, etc. The focus is not merely individual. All pointing at salvation, calling it moksha, mukti and nirvana. Rebirth concepts are common, even if soul exists or not. Metaphors are mostly taken literally. More emphasis on demolishing one’s ego, spiritual awakening, and breaking the cycles of rebirths.

While almost all Eastern philosophies are likely the same even if they use different terms, western one is not romanticism, idealism, and pragmatism only. But why I pushed hard on pragmatic western philosophies is because they have scaled and are measurable. Eastern philosophy is self depreciating, submissive, and provides a goal of after life. While western philosophy is highly applicable and clear.

In simple words, eastern philosophies are about assuming X and western philosophies are actually applicable in day to day life. Liberty, equality, fraternity were no mainstream eastern philosophy ideas.
