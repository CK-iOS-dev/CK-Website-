This is a story about children who don’t want meet expectations off society, and not even the parents. Because they don’t want to be what others want them to be, because they feel different, they think different. They are not afraid to challenge society, they are not afraid to shake the basis of illegitimate foundations, beliefs, and structures.

But it all starts from home, once you are able to hold your views in front of your parents etc, you project independent ability to hold views. Regardless of culture or nation, one holds whatever beliefs they want to. And that is what freedom is about. And here we are to look at how this could backfire, even in this so called modern world.

A child who protested, to hold atheist beliefs in a religious society. To say, the society was religious, but actually the society was not following their religion in the way the religion itself wants them to follow it. But the boy was totally atheist, yet he preached others to be coherent upon whatever they believe. He goes “I cannot force you to believe what I want, yet I will try to make you internally consistent”. He openly said “If a society dislikes cow slaughter, why does it drink milk?” The boy didn’t just dislike incoherent beliefs, he strongly disliked the hypocrisy and inconsistent application.

He was confronted by the society, he had no way to run, he stated it clearly that he’d admit all their beliefs if they have some truth in them, yet they don’t. He said, the society cannot deny him from following what he has found true. And that he will go on a hunger strike, and declares that he doesn’t need anyone’s agreement upon what he holds as his view, he doesn’t need a permission. He denies all authorities, that are in the way to conquer him. He strongly says “No”. Creating a chaotic yet non violent hurdle in the locality.

Eventually, he was force fed, and was beaten by the people for holding what he holds, he said, he won’t stop, and that his biggest weapon is preaching consistency of principles. Which he saw being violated at that very moment.

To avoid further escalation, the boy eventually appears to accept some of the terms, but not for too long. When he grew up, he relocated, and moved to a better nation, tho0ugh not the best.

This story explores how children are treated as inferiors by the so called elders, and appeals that children should get as much respect as possible. No child should be considered small. What is bad for children, is also bad for adults. Yes, there are some physical limitations when you are young, yet it doesn’t mean those are absolute and that adults can do whatever, and children have to bow against their will.

Salute to all the young ones, who have struggled with that.
