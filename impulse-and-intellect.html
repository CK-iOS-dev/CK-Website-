Impulse and Intellect.

Yeah, some trust the impulse, others trust intellect. That doesn’t mean both are equal, and help equally. But it is not necessary for either of the to be always wrong. Life doesn’t work on guarantees, it works on probabilities, you don’t have to figure out which one always works, you have to figure out what probably is a better one.

Impulsive mindset - These are the people with mindset that solely trusts their own impulse, if they feel something is right, it is right, if they feel they want something, thy buy it. They often believe that life is not too long for these purposes and that whatever they do, they must be in the present moment, Often they tells us not to worry about the future. They say, Life is now, here. They often care about “wants”, they care more about feelings. That doesn’t mean the people of such a mindset cannot be successful, they can be. Like Steve Jobs, he is known for being impulsive.

Intellectual mindset - These are the people with mindset that trusts reason, evidence, and logic. They don’t think what they think is right, they don’t simply trust intuition. They analyse, go deeper and deeper, and often become paralysed in analyses. They often overthink. They do think about the future, they plan about it. They still don’t forget uncertainty. They sometimes get into trouble. They often give less priority to wants, and more to needs. This doesn’t mean they will be unsuccessful or successful.

I think neither mindset leads to failure, it is about what you think right, what you feel. But I think you cannot avoid either, but that doesn’t mean you can ever make these traits equally in your life. What do you think - Impulse or Intellect?

I prefer - Impulse and then Intellect.
