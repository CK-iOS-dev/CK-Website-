Apple falls, it tastes worse…

We have heard about the theory of Gravitation by Isaac Newton, that an apple fell on his head, and then he started thinking why an apple falls. The theory started to get developed. While I think Apple hadn’t fallen on his head, that is a story not history.

Apple doesn’t fall, when you throw it into the air. Apple tastes worse than it would otherwise, once you throw it into the air. Eat an apple without throwing it in the air, it will likely taste sweet. If it doesn’t, then you must know that the taste of it could be quite worse than it is now, if you had thrown it into the air.

Now some will argue “Well, we have thrown the apple into the air, and many of them were sweet”. This could also be true, but you are missing something. You haven’t tasted it without throwing it into the air, it would have tasted a lot better.

So,
I am admitting that -
Despite not throwing it into the air, it can taste bad. But it would have tasted worse if you had thrown it in the air.
Despite throwing it in the air, it might still taste sweet, but you have missed the heights of its taste.

I have another example :

A person drank, smoked, and consumed higher sugar content, still lived to 80 years of age. Does that mean we should follow those habits? Such an apple is sweet despite throwing up in the air.

A person didn’t drink, smoke, and consume high sugar content, still lived to 80 years, but they didn’t have gene superiority. So they are a sweet apple, because they weren’t thrown into the air.

A person drank, smoke, and consumed high content, and lived to 55 years of age. That apple was thrown into the air, and it tastes worse.

At first glance the idea sounds stupid and superstitious, but the depth below it is something you can test by your own observation. Now you’d say, it wasn’t about apples. It was about society. No, it applies on Apples.

But the moral is : Don’t throw an apple into the air, it would get dirty as it will fall, 😏.
