Wise people.

How do the wise people live? What inspires them? And why…

We have heard of several wise people, who have been in various occupations, business, philosophy, politics, economics, etc. But I have found some traits common in them despite them being from different domains. Those domains are also interconnected, they are different but each plays some part in the other one.

Those common traits are :

Long term thinking

Considering multiple perspectives

Coherent application of principles

Simple living by cutting the clutter

Not running before the unnecessary goals

These traits are genuinely helpful, regardless of what one thinks, they are independently valuable. Wait, a more precise version adds “If life itself is important to you”. Why? Regardless of any ideology :

1. Long term thinking : It helps us analyse things broadly, and what views we are holding, should be long term, and therefore thinking about them, so that less corrections are needed, it means that you don’t generalise early, you figure things out several times, before a conclusion is reached.
2. Multiple perspectives : Considering one perspective makes one’s views narrower, but narrowness isn’t automatically bad. Yet taking a broad perspective, is something different. It acknowledges alternatives, and weighs the chosen one against them. And done with honesty, it can be even better.
3. Coherent application : The principles you’ve chosen, must not be cherry picked, inconsistent application is dishonest, especially if done consciously. The application must be consistent, no matter what your principles are. If you keep switching your stands in a few days, you are creating a hurdle. One must consider multiple perspectives, and think long term, therefore.
4. Simple living, cutting the clutter : One must not prefer too many unnecessary things in life, sure you can have a few, but not many. If you live simply, your cost of living is automatically lower, relatively to the standards of that society. Clutter must be ditched, and by get rid of it, you make the life cleaner, and lighter.
5. Not running before unnecessary goals : Goal setting should be considered mindfully, and goals should be evaluated before pursuing, one must evaluate if the goals that have been set, are actually worth the struggle. And truly, many of the goals, are unnecessary, and aren’t worth pursuing.

At the end, I would say, certainly some things can actually be debated, and surely I myself can critique this, but the point isn’t that it cannot be criticised. The point is, is it valuable at all? It certainly has something valuable, and the things I have talked about are mostly valuable. I have tried to be honest, though it was not the peak level honesty, but that would have made the essay more complicated.

Wise people.
