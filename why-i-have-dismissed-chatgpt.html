Why I have dismissed ChatGPT?

Since 2022, AI chatbots have come to existence, you prompt in, and they respond. For a few months, the free version of Chat GPT was frozen to 2022. Sometimes it didn’t give response to some things that were sensitive, you couldn’t create image in the free version. But over time, it improved a lot. We got image creation feature, but it was highly inaccurate. However, we are getting closer to accuracy. And now you can talk many sensitive topics on ChatGPT.

Now, I give you an example of how ChatGPT reinforces bias, supports popular narrative, and has to be debated to reach a nuance. While, I’ll not put the screenshot here, you will have the idea otherwise. I tell you what to notice, I talked about “Jallianwala Bagh” and I asked was it a massacre? ChatGPT replied “Many historians say that it was”. It gave me some lines ; The crowd there was innocent. The crowd was unarmed. Firing was unjust.

I said, I am not here to justify the firing happened. Indeed, it was not a norm, even under emergency situations. But we have to understand the other side as well, before we declare the other side is evil. What I criticised about ChatGPT is its methodology. It drew conclusion first, rather than giving facts from both sides. It didn’t raise the question whether the public held any responsibility to follow Rowlatt Act. How could thousands of people be taught a lesson for not following a law?

I also agree that firing wasn’t the usual way of dealing with it all.The complexity wasn’t less either. So a person like Dyer was constrained. That’s why his removal makes sense, because his act couldn’t be justified. But persecution doesn’t, because he was still serving the law. When I repeatedly asked ChatGPT what it would do with roughly 50 troops facing a crowd of around 15,000, it struggled to produce a practical alternative and eventually admitted it might also consider firing.

But let’s look at India’s post independence, Emergency 1975-76 was a worse act. Yet the people still call that lady as Iron Lady. She won a little war. Just like Britain’s Iron Lady Margaret Thatcher, also won a small war but still “Iron”.

My main point is, while ChatGPT could be fine for medical or scientific purposes. Discussing complex issues, ChatGPT fails to make a nuance. I am not saying “Dyer was saintly, he did the right thing”. I am rather understanding how the situation was, to both. I am creating a nuance and balance. Not black and white. This was a mere example. It was more about telling you how ChatGPT is just biased.

At what point does moral judgment require a practical alternative?

Can we call a man a tyrant if we cannot identify a better course of action ourselves?
