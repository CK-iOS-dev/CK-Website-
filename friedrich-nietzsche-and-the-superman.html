Friedrich Nietzsche and the Superman.

Friedrich Nietzsche was a German philosopher from 1899-1944, he lived a life which we can call only survival and not much pleasure. His father passed away when he was only 3-4 years old. His family was christian, his father was baptised. Yet he died, as Nietzsche grew up, he started to oppose christian religion. He was terrified with the death of his father, and questioned why did he die even though he prayed so much?

Nobody gave him satisfying answers. They only made it up, to explain him the death of his father. Nietzsche was sharp, he was good academically. He becomes the professor of philology in his 20s. Though before he was studying theology, the study of religion, which he gave up due to opposition of christianity.

Life was going well for Nietzsche, and he started to write books. A few titles are - Beyond Good and Evil, The Gay Science, and Thus Spoke Zarathustra. He then got mentally ill, retired from his professor’s position.

The Superman : Nietzsche introduces a new concept in his book “Thus Spoke Zarathustra” where he says “God is Dead” and it is the time for Superman. Superman was what people were waiting for, he said “Become one”. It is also called “Übermensch” in German. But the question remains : Was Nietzsche a Superman by his own standards? No.

He was no superman, because I think for a Superman to exist, there must be some ideal world. Nietzsche gave the world the call for Superman, he couldn’t be one. He didn’t lead by example.
