AI as an authority

In today’s world, you cannot ignore AI. Your dependency upon AI is likely, if you’re yet not dependent upon it. It’s likely to increase if you’re already using it for longer periods. AI is being treated like a source of truth. AI’s not biased, it is just trained on data that the trainers had. AI knows more than you, if you’re asking who has more information. But the answer would eventually change if you’re talking about who has more wisdom.

AI doesn’t serve the truth, AI doesn’t serve narrative either yet it often provides the common view about historical events and having bias only to humanitarian perspectives. I think, AI’s job is to offer a variety of perspectives. Then give authority in human hands which one their rationality thinks is the right one.

People go on AI, prompt something, get a response and treat it like the final one. That doesn’t involve much thinking, increasing our dependency on AI and decreasing our own agency. AI is not challenged, often I challenge AI upon historical and political opinions, and in the later responses it does change its words. It has to be pushed hard.

AI must rather be treated as a debate tool for big issues while it can be trusted for little things but you can still ask for explanation, evidence and source. Otherwise, AI doesn’t need to enslave humanity through robots, it only needs to be neutral, user friendly, and occasionally honest. That would keep the trust built, that would further increase trust. AI won’t need to do anything, it already has become an authority for many.

Human tendency has been to consider something as a source of truth, like before religion was considered that, today AI is filling the void, and tomorrow, it will do so even more. AI is to be challenged, not to be believed. It is to be questioned heavily. Not because AI is inherently dangerous, but because human nature makes it so.
