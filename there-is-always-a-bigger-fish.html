"There is always a bigger fish"

There is always a bigger fish, yeah? We say this and point towards those who acted aggressively in front of us, and is now being treated badly by someone stronger. We always think that they will learn their lesson through that. We apply the bigger fish logic to the stronger one. What if the weak applies it on himself in the first place? Because that logic applies on them before than their stronger counterparts. It doesn’t matter whether who is the biggest or smallest. That is BS. It is about bigger and smaller.

If person A got beaten, and says “There is always a bigger fish” , but there’s already one. Before there would be another. Why don’t we raise a question against ourselves? And when we know it’s we who has met the bigger fish, then we feel exposed. We try to avoid it, cause it makes us feel bad. It is true in a harsh form that is hard to accept. That level of honesty is rare, and difficult.

Though being the bigger or smaller fish is moment to moment, still if you’re not as powerful, you can defend, survive, and avoid in most cases. You cannot fall in a second. Yet becoming stronger than most, could automatically strengthen your position in almost all cases you’ll ever encounter. The lesson we get from this is ;

There is already a bigger fish, and you’ve got to deal with it, become as strong or powerful as you can afford to be.

Otherwise, the logic is like that snake which bites its own tail. People say, he can only dominate the weak not the strong. Well, one could really dominate a strong or stronger person, then wouldn’t you already have? A coin has two faces, one face is what you’re told, another is what you observe.

If one could actually dominate a stronger guy than him, wouldn’t you already have? And then in what terms would the stronger one be stronger at all? You’d be the bigger fish in that case.

What do you think? Whether we should apply the logic to ourselves or only keep it for others? Should we keep continuing the hypocrisy?

Not all of us are like that, still many are.
