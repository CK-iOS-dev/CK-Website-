Predictability vs Lies

I am specifically talking about the statecraft model, I am gonna discuss how a state becomes predictable vs a state that keeps lying about the situation to its citizens. I won’t give real world examples, but I would propose scenarios that indicate this.

A state who is lying is a state who promises more than it can deliver, it shows them big dreams but realistically those dreams are near to impossible, but they still want people to believe in those dreams what they don’t know whether they are even achievable or not. Sometimes things are achievable, but they are not achieved on time or just never achieved. It has become an easy play for someone to sell dreams, people to believe in them, and the actual goal is not achieved.

A state that is efficient in achieving goals would either set realistic expectations, or it would achieve quite near to what they say, and that kind of a state is very pragmatic and predictable. Dreams are not just believed in, they are worked upon and achieved. Things get done too fast, meanwhile some states take decades to do what an efficient state completes in a few years.

Predictability is not just about growth. It is also about law enforcement. For example, in a state where speech is restricted due to social order, you know what will happen if you speak freely. Whereas in a state where Free speech is promised, but you don’t know what consequence would you face if you speak freely. In predictable states, the state controls speech. In inefficient states, the society does.
