The fear of failure.

The fear that you will fail, is one of the biggest fears available. At some point of life, we all have to deal with it, somehow in some way. That fear is either overcome or it makes you paralysed, you either move towards success or you don’t even start. The fear comes “What if I fail?” more than the hope “What if I fly?”, somebody said that the probability of your success and failure is equal, I don’t know in which universe that is so.

We cannot know the probability, we cannot know whether we will succeed or not. Even on the probable paths, there are still failures. No guarantee is there for anything. Spend 20 years in education, your job is not guaranteed. You worked very hard, you used all the intellect you had, you can still fail. It is all risky, however, the degree of risk varies at each path. But still, there is no path without risks.

“The greater the risk, the more the reward” one has very less risk in walking but a lot of risks if one travels into space, may be the spaceship loses contact, maybe they run out of oxygen, maybe they won’t land, maybe sun rays would cause cancer to them. Who knows? Still many are excited for this experience. On the other hand, walking is not risk free either, but one assumes that there is greater safety over here. That said, I don’t think that this must be an absolute truth, but the observations I’ve made, state that this rule has some value.

But doing something like that, like going to Space, is actually worthwhile? That remains a separate question, it remains a mystery. Yet the risk was taken, was it worth? It’s a separate question. Whereas one could find satisfaction in walking, as they reflect on themselves. There is no sound in the universe that says, space exploration is obviously better.

How it connects to our broader point? Someone might want to be a millionaire, make a billion dollars corporation, own a large chunk of land, but if that ruins lifestyle, spoils balance, is it actually worth? I quote from someone “Faith is the father of success”. That is what I think that works. If you keep thinking about failure before failure, you might get stability, but not progress. Calculate it, but do it. Fear, after all, is an emotion, not a sound of the whole universe. I mean, use a bit of logic.
