Is Wisdom even real?

We all have some idea of what wisdom might be, we also have examples of wise and noble humans. We have a set of ideas which we call principles, and believe that life is only worth living if it is lived through those principles. Our wisdom comes from those whom we consider wise or a bit from personal experience.

The mismatch is that often whom we call wise and try following their path were wise in the narrow sense. Those wise men kept themselves glued to their own perceptions and preached them as if nothing was left after and their ideas were the final one. They thought that they had reached the truth, enlightenment, and absolute realty.

They preached :

Death is inevitable, don’t be afraid of it. Yet they expressed deep sorrow when they lost a loved one, and justified it by calling it human nature.

Celibacy is ideal, one must practice it but they themselves fell for the desire whenever they got the chance to do so.

Honesty is very important, yet they didn’t hesitate to hide several things they shouldn’t have.

They could justify it all by saying, even if we are not there, you can live by the the principles and be the perfect man but they themselves weren’t.

Wisdom should be practiced and then preached.
