"Degrees will be worthless"

“Degrees will be worthless in the upcoming years”. If so will be true, what would happen to the educational institutions? That’s multi billion dollar industry with constant demand, if so happens, then who’d even validate those big institutions? Who’d spend a lot of money on them? In the age of AI, how can we even think that the things will remain the way they are.

Ofcourse, AI integration is expected almost everywhere, but some say AI is gonna take it over. If AI takes over, then humanity cannot survive without harsh difficulties. Yet I suppose AI integration is a more plausible and almost certain outcome rather than AI taking it over like they describe to call AI a dystopian system. Coming back to degree point, yes, some courses are not as valuable or in demand.

They may not lead to employment but still a significant number of courses are useful and lead to employment. I don’t think that degrees will be worthless, some will be but others will not be. Such a narrative they are spreading, doesn’t appear to be practical or plausible. Sometimes it is just conspiracy, or say most of the time.

With time, things evolve. In different eras, roles start to shift, they done remain the same. The focus then becomes to get things done quickly not eliminate humans entirely. AI integration is unavoidable, and necessary for getting our tasks done quickly and our productivity rises. If we can do a task more efficiently with less effort, why would we not? We always will.

But we should be able to recognise the fault in the system, so that it doesn’t become over relied on AI. We should still think independently, shouldn’t treat AI like an authority. Because if we do, then AI becomes the ultimate authority without even doing anything, and the problem with it is that out acts a bit honestly, which increases our trust even more. The danger is whether we obey AI or debate it.
