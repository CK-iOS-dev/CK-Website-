Consumerism, and us.

We all have noticed how these days we are becoming more and more reliant on internet, processed food, and AI etc. We don’t think most things of our own, we don’t do many things we used to do. Because of the convenience that these things offer. But we are get rid of our own agency by doing so.

Due to addictive social media and AI, we are becoming permanent consumers to the big corporations. They own our time. We eat processed food, we don’t cook much. They own our food. The convenience offered by these modern technologies owns our comfort.

Knowingly or Unknowingly, we are becoming slaves to these things. We are being disconnected from the nature, and developing artificial environments to habitat in. The big corporations treat us no better than mere consumers and are researching to make their products in a way that people stay glued to them. All that is being done for making profit.

Due to development, we have given up religion. We have given up the customs. While we may think that we stand by them, we don’t like our ancestors did. But those customs and traditions were not sacred either. Yet we are in a situation where the lack of meaning in life has become a common issue. I think, consumerism is filling the void, unfortunately.

Once did a philosopher say “I think, therefore I am”. I say “I consume, therefore I am”.

However, I don’t support stagnation either. What I want to say is that these are our tools, they should remain under our control, not wise versa. Today, nobody can stop AI, internet, processed food etc. What we can do is to be prepared to deal with such things in a better way, so that we don’t get drained into the flowing dirty river, we must not go like the herd.

Thanks, for reading.
