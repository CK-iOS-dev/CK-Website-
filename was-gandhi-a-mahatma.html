Was Gandhi a mahatma?

This question is quite interesting, because he is often referred to as Mahatma. He is printed upon the Indian currency, and he is also referred to as the father of the nation, of India. But btw Winston Churchill called MK Gandhi as “A seditious lawyer and now a half naked middle temple fakir. Both figures hated each other.

First Gandhi’s strengths :

1. Non Violence - Gandhi didn’t choose the path of violence to resist the British, most of the times, he remained lawful and was not persecuted. He didn’t just choose Non violence for him, he insisted others to choose it.

2. Literacy - Gandhi was not a typical revolutionary, nor a typical Indian. While you might notice that a few revolutionaries did have educational backgrounds, it wasn’t as unique as Gandhi, as he had studied in England and practiced in Africa, and later back to India.

3. Hunger Strike - Gandhi used this technique to fulfil his demands, he was often known for his hunger strikes, and his influence over making peace in some incidents, though not everything was accepted just because of hunger strikes.

Now the criticism :

1. Racist views - Gandhi held racist views about South Africans, he called them “Savages”, and as per the record, he never publicly acknowledged that his views were racist, also people say he had changed later, but he never apologised. Some believe that he also believed that Dalits should be considered hindus, and didn’t want special representation. He also didn’t demand anything specifically for the oppressed ones.

2) Controversial experiments - Though he believed in celibacy in his later life, to test whether he was actually celibate he often slept with his own nieces.

3) Expensive poverty - He looked poor, but the whole compartment of the train would be booked for him, he drank goat’s milk but that goat ate nuts, and cashews, which were worth significant amount of money. (Osho said)

4) Hypocrisy - While he depicted himself as saintly, he was a flawed man. He was not a villain, but the scrutiny is higher when one claims virtues and doesn’t stand by them. I may not be right saying but it is believed “Gandhi said - Pakistan will be made over my dead body”. He didn’t act upon his vow. From the education, he developed his very intellect, he discouraged it and insisted others for traditional education. While he was opposing a state that took no consent to rule, he didn’t create one that was established by a referendum that could actually tell the people’s consent.

I think, Gandhi was not an absolute tyrant. I think Gandhi was effective in his own place. Does that make him a hero? I don’t think so, and to call him “Mahatma”, prove “Atma” in the first place.
