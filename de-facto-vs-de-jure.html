De facto vs De jure, De facto - De jure

De facto is what is. De jure is what should be.

Let me deepen it ;

“The government has passed a law that nobody will spit in public places”

This is de jure. This is the law, but actually it won’t be followed if there is no punishment after breaking.

“The people don’t spit in public places”

This is de facto, it means that in practice people don’t spit whether a law exists or not.

De jure - De facto ;

It is the law, and it is practiced too.

This was basic explanation. But real life examples are different. We will take look at some major examples of De facto, De jure.
