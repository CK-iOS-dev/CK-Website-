iPhone Pro Max theory

Many people reading this like iPhones, especially the Pro models like Pro and Pro Max. They really cost money. Many of us wanna buy them, but we don’t. We have other commitments as well, especially those living in poor countries. Even those living in rich nations, sometimes find it difficult to buy it. But today we’re looking at the question, which is the most important. Why should you buy it? It costs a significant amount of money.

Some would say, the iPhone Pro Max model looks quite amazing, it’s got great cameras, and it feels good to hold it in hands. Doesn’t it? It does. Others would say, it is nice for videography, gaming and status. It is backed by A19 Pro chip, 12GB ram and vapour chamber.

We see two perspectives, one prioritises experience, another cares about specifications. In simple words, it is beautiful and powerful at the same time. That means it’s got the balance of power and beauty. Ask any of the users, if iPhones were only beautiful not powerful, would you still buy it? Most would go “NO”. Ask again, whether they’d buy it if it was only powerful? Still most would go “NO”.

Nobody is wanting a flashy device, with negligible power. Nobody is wanting a list of specifications. Both are required. When applied onto humans, the conclusion becomes clearer. There must be a balance in life, you can’t be impulsive dumbf*** and you cannot be a cold guy either.

It sounds strange but you can learn something from anything.
