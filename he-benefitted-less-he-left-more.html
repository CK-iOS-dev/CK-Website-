He benefitted less, He left more.

(Satish couldn’t attach it, Samuel did)

Once upon a time, there was a person with broken car (Satish), the car was broken in the sense that it had not been a single cabin like a car is, those were all the parts but separate from each other, making it clutter than a car. But then came a person (Samuel), who wanted to drive a car to visit specific places where cars are preferred, so he jumped straight into it, and he turned those separate parts into one car like a car should be. And then, he started driving, Satish sat in, because he couldn’t be ignored. The person who attached the car, started driving it. He had to take the car to the places he wanted to. He drove the car, the way he wanted to. He’d only take the car to places Satish wanted, if going to that place was mutually beneficial. Satish, at the time, didn’t know how to drive. Satish accepted how he drove. Because Samuel, had experience of driving various cars.

But eventually, Samuel had to go on to other tasks which were very important, and due to lack of time, he couldn’t drive Satish’s car. And Satish had already been asking for him to teach him driving.

Finally, Samuel agreed to teach Satish driving, and when the process was over, he left. Satish, then kept criticising Samuel as he took over the car as if it was his own, and ran for his own purposes.
