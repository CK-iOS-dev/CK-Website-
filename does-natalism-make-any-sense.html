Does Natalism make any sense?

We all in our lives think about building families. Have you ever thought why is it so?According to me, it is because we have been raised in a family. We don’t want to miss the things we were raised in. That’s why we make families.

What does it have to do with natalism though? if you had that question in mind, my friend you’re forgetting that children are a crucial part of a family. And what we’d look at is how much sense does it make, morally, socially, economically and naturally 😆. So let us take a look at this.

Morally, now this is where it gets interesting. We live in a society that respects individual consent these days. Giving birth is consent from whom? Whatever, but the creator can’t ask creation to create it. So how do we deal with consent? Well, a child is not a typical creation anyway. A child is someone who’d eventually start to feel things even before they can speak or walk. You can’t call it a normal creation because the outcome is exactly what you once were, you should think like it’s you not your creation, then you can empathise with what you might call your creation. Being straightforward, no it doesn’t make sense morally.

Socially? You would see many of your friends make families and have children, this would likely make of think of doing exactly like that. Because the people yo live in, you become like them. You’re likely to have a bit more social acceptance by having children. Yeah, it does make a bit of sense socially.

Economically? well, this might become controversial again but children surely are an expense we add willingly (or unwillingly sometimes). But not only it costs us our money but also more energy and time. It might only make sense for a few wealthy individuals. I would say for most of us, it isn’t convenient either. There was once a time when having as many children as possible made bigger and wealthier families, it now the opposite.

Naturally? Yeah, humans just like any other species are meant to pass their genes and DNA to the next generation, not only that, children can also bring happiness. You can release your stress through playing or talking to them.

But I think ;

Natural is not fair always.

What “might be” is not guaranteed.

Forcing things without consent shouldn’t be considered that normal, especially regarding life.
