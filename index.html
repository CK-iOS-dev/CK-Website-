The Takae Kun A doctrine for modern masculinity. Takae Kun, a boy who grew up watching the rise of feminism, women rights, and was taught that human beings are equal, regardless of who they are, male or female. But Takae Kun was a male, he couldn’t have accepted something that would have weakened his position. Over time, he develops a new system which was highly based on the traditional patriarchy, it was similar, but not the same. Before proposing it, Takae Kun thinks about something: The system/world could have the following possibilities of dynamic - The world would be male dominated, it means that the number of men in power or most fields would exceed than that of women. Or The world would be dominated by women, in the same way. Or The world would have equal or near equal numbers, and strength of both genders. That’s it. Now he comes to development of his own system, but let’s get a flash back of who Takae Kun is. Takae Kun is a 45 years old man, who has built his own house, and holds pride in owning it, often describes the house payment years (15 years) as a war. He’s won it. But Takae Kun is single. Takae Kun Doctrine He is a proud man. He disregards equality concepts. Treat fairly, not equally. He gives a Cold War example, in Soviet Union, the commoners were equal. But what was the standard of their lives? And in the US, the commoners were not equal, yet they had a better standard of living, though it differed significantly. But then he talks about Britain, he describes how that nation calculates on defence, has nukes, permanent member of the UN, embedded in alliances, and substantial historical influence. In that nation state, the standard of living was also maintained, healthcare and education were ensured for free. He uses this example to describe: A nation state with equality, but not high living standards. A nation state with high living standards for even commoners, but not equality. A nation state with high living standards for commoners, though relatively less inequality to the second. He describes that his household cannot provide equality, because he worked hard to build his house. And whoever lives in there, cannot be equal to who built it. They haven’t yet worked hard. He makes exception for lodgers, because they pay for living. He also describes that his household cannot be the United States either, where immense inequality can persist. However, he doesn’t discourage it completely. He rather chooses the Great Britain model. Let me describe further - Takae Kun, is the sole owner of the house. His spouse which is necessarily a female, won’t have any ownership right over the house. Takae, would not cover her expenses. He would not take the obligation to defend her physically necessarily. She cannot share room with the Kun, unless she has the special permission. Kun won’t ask her to. She cannot cook meals for the Kun, unless permitted. She cannot shout at the Kun, let physical violence alone. She can appeal and petition, but not “order” or “force”. The Kun has the right to carry a black thick stick, as a tradition he himself started. The Kun won’t give his salary to her, the Kun would give a little amount occasionally if he wishes. Takae Kun, the sole owner. He would be not called by name. Not “Takae” “Takae”. He would have a special title, the Kun. No nicknames or name allowed, unless permitted. This sounds way too much by modern standards. But it doesn’t mean his female partner is suppressed. She has basic rights which cannot be denied: She must have a personal room, and guaranteed basic things like food and clothing. The Kun secretly retains a key for that room, for emergency. She has the right to make money, and Takae demands not even a penny as charity. However, a loan won’t be denied if needed. She cannot be shouted at, that violates the peace of the household, but if it is broken already by her, then the case is different. That applies as an emergency law. She won’t have a common name, she would be called the “the Miss” or other title, if approved by Takae. She can give suggestions without fear, but those suggestions would not be final. Takae openly says: By agreeing to these terms, she gets free house, guaranteed basics (if she doesn’t earn), right to earn, her own space. That’s it. If those aren’t enough, I don’t care. They remain how they are. Takae’s view on human equality: It would be insufficient to consider an entire race, sex, nation as either equal or superior than the other. And that is subtle, it has to be judged case by case. He says “Men = Women, is normative truth” not the one which is defensible regardless of the norm. He doesn’t want women to have rights. He wants one type of rights, the human rights. He strongly believes that if women slapped a man, that’s generally not viewed like domestic violence.
