Reality of Education

UNESCO institute of Statistics claims that over 86% of the population in the world are literate. That number shocked me, I couldn’t imagine how could these many people be literate and I don’t think these many at all. But this criterion is flawed, it focuses on barely reading and writing. I am not claiming it, just go on AI and challenge it. Ask if this large percentage has even had 10 years of education or not. The threshold is very low, it just means that figures are larger than they should be.

There are two kinds of literacy :

Statistical - Exaggerated.

Functional - What actually is.

You are wrong even if you think that 80%+ out of 8 billion hold even 10th certificate, they don’t. I am essentially talking about adult population, because you’d obviously find 2 billion of old and very young people. We are not counting on them. Out of the total adult population worldwide :

1. Secondary education - 70% people on this planet, have crossed that threshold. But secondary education is too basic to be considered educated.

2. Senior Secondary - Roughly 50% cross that threshold, but they still don’t hold a degree. Not too basic, not standard either.

3. Undergraduate - That drops to mere 8%, and that just means that holding a basic degree is some kind of luxury.

4. Postgraduate - These are merely 2%, post graduate (masters) is even more of a luxury.

5. Doctorate - Roughly 0.1%.

But even those who hold a degree could be fools, and we cannot say about those who are only “basic”. Of course, there can be so called illiterates who might sometimes be more intelligent, but that doesn’t mean majority are. The reality of education is devastating. And while it may seem that our developed nation has 99% literacy, that is not actually so. It is far less. But since the criterions are small, most things fit most thresholds, but those are flawed ones. When they say 86% or 99%, they basically mean primary education.
