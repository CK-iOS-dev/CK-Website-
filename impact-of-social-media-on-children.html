Impact of Social Media on Children

Social media apps are today accessible by almost everyone on this planet now, the world has become narrower due to social media. Today, we simply know what’s happening in other parts of the world. We get news instantly. We get a variety of information. Social media doe shave beneficial aspects as well. But we have to look closely how it impacts the children.

Today, many children/teens can access content that isn’t appropriate to their age, toxic behaviour, vulgar language and explicit content can be accessed by anyone in no time. Thats why it becomes worthwhile to once think about how we can protect our children from this stuff, we do have options like parental control to limit a child’s access to such content, but often we cannot limit a child from figuring such things out from other children and in fact some adults. The fault isn’t merely of social media but adults often watch such content, they shouldn’t though.

Most teens have already accessed such content through social media, and how can we forget how addicted the generation is, regarding social media. This behaviour is not healthy, and shouldn’t be carried continuously. Otherwise, it would have severe impact upon the society.

In some countries, social media is banned for children or the government is considering it, for children’s safety, beware if it’s not some kind of surveillance, then your choice. But kids usually use their parent’s phone and their parents accounts. Not their own. Banning sounds useless.

My suggestion is : Adults should themselves limit their social media use, set parental controls, and have a friendly relationship with children so that risks are minimised.
