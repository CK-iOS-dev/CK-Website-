Reward for Honesty.

You might have heard many stories about how Honesty is rewarded. How being Honest is an important thing. Why Honesty plays a great role in this world. They pick a certain folklore character, build up a story with certain conditions and build it up to encourage you to be Honest.

I say it is all false. They just want you to be honest, and they are not telling you the reality. Let me give you an example. Let us say relatives have come to your house, they want a celebration but you somehow appear to not be ale to do so. They ask “Can’t you do it?” if you be honest “I can’t”. That weakens your positions very often, unless the relatives are kind. Let us say, a person is about to die, they ask if they could make it, if you be honest and say that they’d die, you’ll get no reward.

If a journalist gets honest about the government, in most nations they are not allowed to survive. If you be honest about what you don’t like, you are hateful. If you be honest about what you like, people often don’t respect you.

If you honestly question things, you find mostly meaningless ones. Honesty is not rewarded, what is rewarded is what suits the powerful. Not you. In that case, honesty doesn’t’t remain honesty, it becomes cherry picking of it.
