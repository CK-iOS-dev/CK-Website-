Not the only theory

We have all argued with ChatGPT, we have made consistent arguments, we have been internally consistent, made rational and logical points. Those points seem to be very true to us. Because they are backed by our observation, backed by our witness, and the evidence we are aware of. But ChatGPT never fully accepts anything. No ideology, no arguments, no philosophy. But why? What is the benefit of arguing so hard and then get a response like “Well, this is one of the perspectives”, as if there was a marketplace of perspectives.

Why should we accept anything at all? And make rational arguments later, to just hear “This is not the only theory, there are others as well”. Why should we choose any? We are told no theory is universal, no truth is absolute. What defines absolutism? What defines universal truth?

What does it mean? There are subjective interpretations, not truth. We are here for truth, maybe that is because we were told to prefer truth and truth only, over any lies. Yet those lies seem compelling, those lies feel like truth to many, those lie make people’s lives comfortable. But it isn’t about comfort, it is about what is reality, and what we should do.

After researching all that, I don’t find it satisfying when it is said “It is not the only theory” that is an easy escape argument, it may not entirely be irrational, but it doesn’t require much reason, it can be said when you are run out of arguments, but I am not saying that it doesn’t have any value. It does hold value, but we are here for truth not mere argument. We argue for truth.

And that, existence of other theories doesn’t mean that the theory I am proposing is a false one, it doesn’t mean all theories are equal, it doesn’t mean that criticism necessarily means that there is actually something wrong with the theory, it may not be. A theory must be backed by ;

1. Evidence.

2. Witness.

3. Logical consistency.

We want truth, not a market place of it. We strongly believe in that, and will continue to believe so, as we have found something compelling. Very much so.
