Does Bad weigh more than Good?

Now this is a thing we should realise before we take big decisions of our life, because the results could be good or bad, we need to evaluate what weighs more, and what doesn’t. How do we figure that out? Let’s observe reality, here I am giving you real world examples to make sure you understand it in depth.

Let us say, you earned a £100,000 , you are happy with that. You have plans to spend it, invest it, and extract fun out of it. But what if a disaster comes upon, and you lose that money in it. What do you then do? you work hard and make another £100,000 in a few months or years. You may be thinking that you have gained £100,000 again and it is net zero again. But that is not like that, your time, your effort, your grief was the extra price you paid for it.

If a person dies, it doesn’t come back. If something is destroyed, it takes unnecessary time to create it again. That requires you to repeat the process anyway. After destruction, the creation of what exactly was, is ambiguous, and uncertain. For example, Germany can never reach the position it had in 1913, no matter the abstract economic prosperity, no matter the modernisation.

What people ignore in recoveries is that, recovery was unnecessary. You are working hard again, to reach what you already were. For example if a person murders somebody, their crime is irreversible, all you can do against harm is to prevent future harm, that is the most prudential and plausible approach.
