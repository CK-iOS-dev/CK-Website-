Equal or Effective?

We have been taught to prioritise equality because that is fair, it is always good to be equal. Whereas, we notice that we are equal in nothing. We see everything we have is unequal, only sometimes the minimum is the equal for all. But mostly, we all live above that criterion where it all feels fair and equal.

My argument points toward something deeper - whether we should prioritise equality or whatever whoever deserves? Yet I think, it matters negligibly who thinks what is right or wrong, the existence works upon a different thing, survival is what everyone prioritises whether more or less. Survival of the system, in the minimum working conditions.

They say “It just works” but that way a car with broken seats and the weakest condition to perform, still works on paper. It works but they don’t tell for whom it works. And obviously not for all, not even for those who keep ignoring it. And those who have already realised it, it works like nothing.

If we think that everyone will get equally but everyone will contribute as per their ability, what is equal in it? It is another form of inequality, that is almost never highlighted. If we believe, everyone will pay as per their ability but only get what everyone else will, what is even the benefit of giving in more?

It doesn’t need to be equal to be effective, it doesn’t need to be effective to be equal. Does it?
