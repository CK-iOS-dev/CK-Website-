The Game Theory

Developed by which theorist? To me that doesn’t matter. But it is quite of an interesting theory. It is logical and mathematical. It tells us a lot about strategic decision making. And as it sounds, it assumes the world to be a simulation or simply a game, that can be won.

You can easily get the definition searched and read about it. The explanation is hard. I will not cover each and every thing. I will only tell you the reality, then let you decide whether it feels like a game in reality or does it not? Because that is quite more important, than mere looking at what anyone can, in a few seconds.

Is it all a game? I tell you why it feels plausible. You often feel no better than a game character. You wake up, play the game and sleep. It feels like there are other players as well, because you have recognised the constant competition, and the race. You will now think that if it is a game, what are the rules? Mainly there are two main rulers who make rules ;

The society.

The Government.

If you think you will win by following the rules, I don’t know if you will. Let me give a specific example, as you all know that students come to first world nations for studying and they never go. Most students of 1st world countries don’t go in other countries to study. Most students come from the poorer parts of the world. They cannot come if they follow rules strictly :

1. They show funds only for the required period, usually they don’t have much.

2. Usually, the income in their countries is multiple times less than the university fee, even if you talk annually. Yet many still opt 3-4 year courses.

3. They are only allowed to work 20 hours, but due to bad economics, they work in the shadow for twice or thrice the limit.

Many have broken the rules, and have done the three things listed above. This creates a bad precedent, for genuine applicants when they see how rule breakers have been successful to break the rules, and only a few unfortunate ones are ever punished.

This was one particular example, but there are countless domains where the rules of the game are broken, and most of the time those rule breakers have better gains than those who abide to the rules.

I’m not telling you what to do, I’m telling you what happens. But there is something ;

Let’s say you’re playing football or Cricket. You abide to the rules, you are much safer than those who break the rules of the game, because bad fortune won’t punish you for breaking rules at least. You can still win a game, even if you strictly abide to rules. And it is not optimistic or impractical.

I personally advice “Abide by the rules”.
