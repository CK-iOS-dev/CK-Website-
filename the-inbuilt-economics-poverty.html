The inbuilt Economics (poverty)

We have all read economics and we know several economic concepts, and we know them to be true. But today I’m talking the inbuilt economic structure, that enforces poverty. We might have seen poverty criterions by the world bank. Those criterions are quite narrower. Real poverty persists above the levels they indicate, they understate poverty so governments can say that only a few are poor.

I heard someone say “You are poor, relative to what? If you compare, you’ll always be poorer< that is why the criterions of the world bank are okay” yet I have to say that one cannot still consider those criterions right, as they barely focus on calorie consumption and certain dollars a day. Education, health care, debt are never considered.

Why I am saying poverty is inbuilt? We can eradicate poverty within a few years, but if everyone is in fair condition, then who’d they point at and say “If you don’t study, you end up poor”. The system has to keep poverty, otherwise, unlimited money could be printed. This statement is partially true.

But actually, money doesn’t mean anything if there aren’t enough resources, and that is a major problem. Poverty will remain, and we will hear about eradicating it, because the issue isn’t as simple as $3 a day, it is way beyond.
