Binary Ethical Evaluation

This is a new phenomenon. And it is the one worth considering. Binary means “yes” or “no”, Ethical means “good’ or “bad”, and Evaluation means “calculation”. You better understand the context now, let’s look at our example :

Winston Churchill - The prime minister of the United Kingdom (1940-45) and again (1951-1955), he was a flawed man. He was not a saint. He never claimed to be one. He had a strong desire of being influential. He had been in various businesses, from being a soldier to writer to a politician and an artist in meantime. He was not a fool, just because he was flawed and validation seeking. He was blunt and direct. He led Britain to victory, when all hopes were dead, he became one. Now his statues are being taken down, he is being removed from the Fiver (£5 note).

Why? Because people find his views as racial and controversial, however, those were views. People often hold him accountable for Bengal famine (1943) and say “Hitler for jews, Churchill for Indians”. They say he is responsible for the death of about 4 million people.

But the number is debated, it is assumed to be somewhere from 800,000 to 4,000,000. I won’t believe any of the numbers, I would call any of this number as huge. Yeah, both are huge enough.

There are a few reasons why I think he wasn’t the causer of it :

World War 2 - You are leading a nation in the most dangerous war of all time, the threat is existential, not little. You are an island fighting a continentally dominant nation, so you have to use your empire in the best way possible. That’s what exactly was done.

Food in Britain - Britain was a dependent nation for most of its food, but due to attacks on ships it became harder to get 45 million people in that nation to be fed till the war persists and is not won, strict rationing was implemented.

Japanese attack - Japan had been expanding in the eastern part of Asia, and had already captured Burma (Myanmar), that cost a lot of food, and the situation was tense for India now, and so was for Britain. Bengal was very close to Burma.

Storm and Cyclone - Even the nature didn’t show mercy on the Bengalis that unnecessarily were gonna lose their lives, it destroyed the harvest of that particular year.

Hoarding - No matter if the local government said “Things are under control” , hoarders knew that the war is hitting India as well, they bought the limited stock in large amounts and the situation got more tense.

Local Government and the Data - Local government was responsible but due to corruption, they didn’t tell accurate data to higher authorities. Which exaggerated the issue that could be solved.

Debate, not denial - Churchill and his cabinet didn’t deny relief straight away, it was debated, in what amount should it be provided, relief was partially provided.

How was it then recovered from? For three major reasons -

The British - Indian Army took direct control of the situation, getting things on track without corruption and understatement.

Substantial relief was provided, Churchill didn’t deny it by only relying on harvest.

Harvest, that year, was fair to the people.

The prevention was due to several factors not a single. We only see 800,000 - 4,000,000 died. But we never ask : What would I have done? What would these judges who are passing judgements would do? Why doesn’t anyone imagine himself in Churchill’s shoes.

We see those who died, but we don’t see those who were saved from dying.

Binary Ethical Evaluation has a formula : Just like 65 + -15 =50 and not 65 + -15 without a result, the formula states the positive impact - negative impact = overall impact.

By this “BEE” criterion, Churchill is a net positive figure by a large margin.
