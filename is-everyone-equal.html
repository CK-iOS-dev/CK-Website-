Is everyone equal?

How can we know that? From observation, logic, and evidence. But also, we have to decide in which respect are we analysing because the word equality isn’t a single kind of equality, we’d have to observe it in nature, find out whether it is true, we have to dive into the society to figure it out, we would have to look at the political structure.

Before concluding whether evidence supports it or not, we’d look into 3 equalities to figure that out ;

1. Natural Equality

2. Social Equality

3. Political Equality

4. Moral Equality

Naturally : There are different genes which lead to different heights, immunities, strength etc. Genes are not equal at all. If we look at the animal kingdom, there one survives after it kills another, where doesn’t equality go? Why is an elephant too giant and a mouse too tiny? Why is one man stronger than another? I see no equality in nature.

Social : The norm is becoming mainstream, it hasn’t yet. Sure, to some extent it is possible for the society to be more equal. I am not wanting absolute equality, but I am saying it just cannot achieve the possible one, it would rather consider current one as the most practical. It currently exists in negligible form. A father is always a father, elders are always elders, rules for children and adults are very different even though the harm being caused is potentially equal. No, it doesn’t exist in the society.

Political : On paper it seems your vote has value equal to everyone else, yet the structure is majoritarian not equal. You will automatically have more say if you fund campaigns, or you are quite influential. Despite being elected by humans, an mp holds very limited power while the power of a PM seems infinite, though it isn’t but the difference is very huge. No political equality, it is partial of the partial.

Moral : What do I say here? A person is viewed from various eyes, with various moral standards, to some he is a villain, to others he is a hero to others, and he is a nobody for many. Not equal at all.

From all that I have observed, rationalised, and have seen the evidence for, I see no equality at all.
