Fight or Flight

We all have heard about this thing, it asks whether we should face it or run away. Whether it is little decision or life itself. Often “Fight” is considered to be the option that is worth considering, because it requires courage, it involves risks, and the outcome is often uncertain whether you’d win the fight or lose it. Whereas, “Flight” is considered cowardice, even if the outcome is likely that you escape. It is still not considered an “Honourable” alternative.

But I want to challenge this norm. If one can safely escape a danger, but they are not escaping that due to their perception. Because they have been told that choosing “flight” is an option cowards consider, courageous consider “fight”. I heard someone say something on it when asked by his friend : What should the person do if there is a wall in front of us? That wall cannot be broken. He replies to his friend, One should keep striking his head into that wall. His friend says that one would die if they keep doing that. He replies back, No matter if one dies, still Fight.

Those are the things we like to hear, because they show us courage. But does that mean they are the right ones? I don’t think courage is always right. And that flight can often make you escape unnecessary situations and useless escalations. I want you to weigh both, and see which one is a better option. “Fight” is what one has to do in a situation where there is no other option left. “Flight” is the option what you should choose otherwise.

It is not about honour, it is not about courage. It is about how many options you have available. Which ones work better for you, what is necessary at the moment, what can be realistically done. Never make your perception put you in a trouble you cannot afford to come out of. It is about using your intellect rather than sticking to mere labels. Because to those who have been hit by “Fight” severely, don’t think about honour and courage anymore. You can think about them only when you are alive and only if you are in a position to think so.
