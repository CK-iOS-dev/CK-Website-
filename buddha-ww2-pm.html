Buddha as WW2 PM

Disclaimer : It is a hypothetical scenario to rethink a counterfactual in an extraordinary situation like World War 2. People might think that “Peace” philosophy doesn’t work here. But we’d still assume that Buddha was alive and had the opportunity to write history at the moment.

In 1940, Neville Chamberlain feels like failure and resigns. Buddha becomes the Prime Minister now. Now he’s got the choice whether to continue the war struggle or make peace. Buddha was a very non violent type of person, he chose peace. Don’t assume he’s naive, he is quite aware that Adolf Hitler can break it any moment. But Buddha took the chance. Buddha had a theory “The Germania Factor”. He had thought, Hitler is a strong opponent of Soviet Union, and he’d likely want to take Soviet Union. “The Germania Factor” is a theory he proposed, it meant that if Germany somehow succeeds to win over the USSR, the reality would be different than what’s perceived. He strongly argued that by invading the USSR, Germany won’t gain all of the USSR’s strength, it won’t be able to maintain the same strength it had before the “Operation Barbarossa”. The human loss would be catastrophic on both the sides, as the USSR won’t fall so easily, even without allied support. The human loss would be significant, and could possibly anger the Germans themselves that the loss is more than the gain, when their sons, their brothers would lose lives unnecessarily. However, he doesn’t bet on this alone. Buddha made peace, and stopped referring the Axis powers as enemies. Buddha was scrutinised heavily, and was called a traitor and pro Nazi PM. Buddha was heavily asked for resigning. Buddha denied, and insisted to explain. Buddha holds an address to the nation - My dear fellows, I know that the geopolitical situation around the world is becoming unusual, however, we must not respond by aggression. The losses can be severe, we must hold on to restraint. And it is obvious, that once somebody attacks our nation or our widespread empire, we will defend effectively. However, we don’t want an unnecessary enmity, we want harmony all over the nation and its overseas empire. I could have done significant propaganda, like they did in the First World War, but I didn’t. We, leaders, can easily use propaganda, but who will die? Your lives are quite valuable, and that the response to an aggression that can be avoided, even in temporarily, cannot be aggression but peace. We shouldn’t demonise the ones who some of us consider the enemies. I strongly insist the nation, and the empire to go on as it has, but we’d not use the trademark strategy. Peace, all over, peace for the empire, peace for the commoner, peace, not a war. If you want me to resign cause I’m insisting to save our own people’s lives, and considering that they are very valuable, and do I have to say “We will defend” because that’s such a very obvious thing. I chose to be honest and wise, you now choose to be fair and nice. Not to me, but to yourself. Your lives are important.

The people didn’t understand it all, but they understood something important, one can call Buddha anything but he’s truly saving our lives. He’s not straining our expenses straight away, he’s also ensuring defence is obvious.

Buddha strongly believed that the Axis strategy was unsustainable by structure. Germans were moving on MEFO bills. Lack of resources was the main constraint of the Axis. And the Germania factor was significant, Buddha explains to his strategists :

If for example in 1940 :
Germany’s economic output = £100
Military Strength - 10M
Tanks - 8,000

Soviet Union
Economic Output - £100
Military Strength - 10M
Tanks - 8,000
Then if in 1943 or 1945, Germany wins then the new superstate “Germania” won’t be Germany + Soviet Union strength combined.
Don’t assume then :
Germania
Strength - 20M
Economy - £200
Tanks - 16,000
The reality would be far more nuanced. Not like you think.

Meanwhile, Buddha’s Britain would prepare itself during that time, would focus on economic output, and cities won’t be destroyed, no rationing would be required. If Germany actually wins over the USSR, it won’t be able to wipe out the Britain and its empire. And the US would still be alive.

This was focused on Germany heavily, what about Italy and Japan?

Firstly, Italy could’t dare doing much, without the German support, since Germany is focused on the USSR, Italy will follow Germany or simply create more problems for it, if it attacks Britain. Buddha believed that if Japan or Italy attacks any British territory, he’d have jumped in. But Buddha knows that’s an emergency circumstance. Japan has to deal with China properly, before it might eye over the British territories in Asia. Since peace was made with Germany, Japan also has lesser of an excuse to attack the British. But Buddha chose neutrality with the Axis, even if Japan attacks, the responses would be defensive, and a bit retaliatory, not exactly wanting the defeat of the Japanese Empire. Treaties, ceasefires, and retreats would be considered. Buddha believed Japan would focus on getting China firstly, if it is genuinely serious.

Buddha was making practical bets, rather than a magical peace. Pragmatism doesn’t always mean aggression, it doesn’t mean dragging the nation into war, before it has been asked for one. Buddha reminds his cabinet :

In 1914, the conflict could be avoided. Though a treaty would be dishonoured like one is, now. The 100 year treaties, both of them would be dishonoured.
But if you change the perspective, you’d see someone else’s promise has to be fulfilled by somebody else. Someone’s pen, someone’s life. The asymmetry is not ignorable.
So if Britain hadn’t entered WW1, Central Powers’ victory won’t be certain or guaranteed. And remember even in that case, the US would still be alive. The Germania factor does work, even there.
You cannot think :
German Empire defeats France and Russia with support from Austro Hungarian and Ottoman Empire, and they’d still be as strong. You cannot think they’d be twice as strong. But perhaps we will be.
Let that alone, it’s history. But today is in our hand, let’s change the strategy, let’s change the strategy.
