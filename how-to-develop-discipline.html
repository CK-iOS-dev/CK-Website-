How to develop Discipline?

Have you ever thought about this? Most of us think that we don’t have any discipline, that’s why we are achieving way less than we can achieve. We often lack consistency and feel lazy. Why is it so? and did our ancestors feel as lazy as we do? Let’s take a look at our ancestors’ behaviour first.

Ancestors’ behaviour ;

Life was not easy, comfort was rare. Even they rested whenever they got the opportunity, energy conservation was necessary. Evidence shows humans have always preferred comfort and reward whenever possible. Their environment simply didn’t allow much inaction.

There’s also a cognitive bias here: people in every era tend to think the current generation lacks discipline. Ancient writers complained about youth being soft or distracted thousands of years ago.

How to develop discipline actually? We shouldn’t feel bad for not having discipline but what we should do is to develop it. Discipline won’t develop on its own, it won’t develop immediately. Don’t lose your motivation. Never, Never, Never kneel down and think you’re unable. Because if it doesn’t exist now, doesn’t mean it won’t ever exist.

Develop small habits, stick to them and later shift to bigger habits. Tell your brain that once you commit, you complete. You start and don’t stop until you done reach the destination. You don’t fail, you learn more. Nobody was born disciplined. It develops over time, so stick with it, give it some time.
