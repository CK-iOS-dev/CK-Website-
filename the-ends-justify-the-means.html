The ends justify the means

We have heard about something called “the ends justify the means”, it simply means that the justification of the action relies upon the outcome it produces. No matter if one intends to do evil but accidentally happens something good. No matter if one intends to depo fair but evil happens due to constraints.

Firstly, how do we know what a person had actually intended something that could be the opposite of what has happened? To analyse that, we have to look into their hearts. Nope!! We have to look into their actions, for a example, if a leader was acting to prevent a famine that could starve millions, but due to a global conflict, regional conflict, improper information, and the culprit of regional government, he fails to do so, but later provides immediate relief what is his fault? I am not saying he really wanted to do what he did, because of sympathy or empathy, but still his actions show his intention not what his heart felt or even the outcome.

On the other hand, a person who solely wanted to shine his politics, wanted influence, didn’t really care what is right or wrong, but he strongly talked about what is right and wrong, developed an ideology that puts people back into stone ages, has controversial experiments, never apologises for being racist, despite being from a race that was a victim of racism itself, is celebrated as saintly and kind, and is given all those honours he should not have, people regard him, not because he was actually effective but he played on the narrative very well.

In case 1, the person’s actions indicate the intention was not of what happened.

In case 2, the person’s actions were again don’t indicate his intention was of what happened.

Yet one’s intention was not bad, another a hypocrite selfish one.
