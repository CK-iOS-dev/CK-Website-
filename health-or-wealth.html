Health or Wealth?

Some say Health is the most important, because protecting the body from disease is the most important task of one’s life, because it preserves the lives. Others say, Wealth is necessary for you to be healthy, you can then eat healthy food without worrying about the bill, you can train in the gym without worrying.

Let’a look at it,

If you have health, you are fit, you can get many tasks done. You can run, you can jump, you can walk several miles in a day. Your belly is not obese, you have 6 packs there, you don’t feel embarrassed of it. You feel good due to your fitness. But your income is less, you struggle to meet even basic expenses, you consider working overtime due to that, but you are in trouble, because you don’t want to trade your health for it.

If you have wealth, you are chubby person, you can eat healthy, train, and stay in contact of a personal medical team. But you don’t have much time, how can you then train? You don’t have normal dinners, because you often go to parties, how can you even eat healthy? If you optimise for work life balance, you cannot get the growth you are desiring.

I must say, too much obsession is the problem. Sometimes health has to be prioritised. Sometimes wealth has to be prioritised. It is about which one you have less? To a poor person, wealth matters more. To rich, healthy might. Yet a basic maintenance of both health and wealth is important, even if one is more important for you.

Basic health maintainance :

1. Don’t eat trash.

2. Exercise a bit.

3. Sleep (7-9 hrs).

4. Follow a basic protocol

Basic wealth maintainance :

1. Don’t spend impulsively.

2. Save even if little.

3. Build a new skill.

4. Have a budget you must follow.
