Why still have some humanism?

Humanism is an ideology that believes humanity is the religion of humans, and that all humans must be treated fairly and all should be loving and caring, and that serving humanity is the goal, one must give up other religions and adapt it for the welfare of humanity. And believe that it is good for humanity.

Do I agree with that? I don’t agree. In fact, I have anti-natalist stance that believes that life itself is the root cause of all evils, this doesn’t mean it encourages death or death is good. It is simply that one must not give birth to offsprings. That is because life is full of complexities and problems, we cannot decide it for other whether we should give it to new ones.

I don’t believe that one must make sacrifices or give donations if they don’t will so. In the name of humanism, they want you donate money, make sacrifices for humanity, die for them, feel guilty for others, because that is not your duty. You should not be obliged to do anything at all.

Still, I say one should have some humanism because I prefer law and order, I want a society where not even the weakest will be allowed to fall, I value honesty, I admire truth. Yet I have no commitments, I am not a person of morality, I prefer legality, cold pragmatic, beaurocratic procedures. Yeah, I am a bit of statecraft person but I believe to some extent one should think about the fellow humans.
