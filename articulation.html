Articulation.

We all speak, don’t we? I mean most of us do, and I wish all can. While most of us can speak, whether we speak clearly, grammatically accurate, without fumbling is what also matters. You see, some people start speaking and often repeat words like “You know” “You see” “Um” “uh” “Guess what”. Those are used to fill the gaps that come into the conversation, it means you are missing a little bit, eventually you speak after that.

Articulation, that means you are saying exactly what you want to say without having filled those gaps with unnecessary “You knows”. Saying exactly what you want is a skill in itself, and it is not a skill even most famous people have. When asked, they reply they fumble, fill gaps, say “You know” “Guess what” liken words, because they are too busy and they have to speak fast. They speak like that under stress.

In reality, they just don’t improve the way they speak. When you don’t try improving, you never improve, and you justify it by saying “I didn’t have time” “Oh I have loads of other businesses” “Who notices that?” those are excuses, we are not targeting absolute articulation. We are targeting Articulation as much as possible. It doesn’t mean we will be perfect, it just means we will define what is perfect.

A few advices to improve your speech for Articulation :

1. Listen carefully.

2. Speak a bit slowly.

3. Avoid saying gap filling words.

4. Practice speech over and over.

5. Stay consistent, you won’t learn it in a few months.
