To all those "Live forever" guys

Once it was considered that death is inevitable, certain, and the unavoidable ultimate truth, and is still believed to be exactly the same. Why? Death could be optional, be prevented. Perhaps achieving a state of immortality is almost impossible, yet we can absolutely say that with time human life expectancy could exceed the mark of 150 and beyond. Just for a moment imagine living for 150 years.

Especially you!! You the reader. What does that feel like? Exciting or boring? For plenty of people the answer is gotta be “Yes, it does excite, rather than boredom”. Because then, you feel as if you have 2 lives to live, given that you stay active till your last years. Otherwise, you cannot afford to spend 80-90 years on bed alone. Lol. Though that tells us something, that if we live that much we might live longer past 2-3 of our generations, while we’d easily become great*5 or *6 grandparents. If only us get that capability.

Yet then scenario in which, we might only remain active for usual years while dependent on others for 80-90 years, it would make quite a less of sense. Then we’d call it boring etc. What that teaches us is that, living X years is only worth if you’re able to enjoy it. Youre not counting years alone.

That’s why, I say to those obsessed with living forever, must think about it. Still normal people would die at normal ages. Because that requires money, discipline, and a boring routine. That’s what most don’t have available. Yet I’d say, this much of restraint is of what worth? Living for the sake of living? Lol.

I recommend following healthy habits so you die peacefully not because it would make you immortal. What are your thoughts?
