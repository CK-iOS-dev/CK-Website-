Does Perspectivism make sense?

Hmm, when discussing philosophy or politics etc, there occur lots of perspectives, some are considered truthful while others are treated less valuably depending upon a person’s ideology, and way of thinking.

While it is common for different individuals to have different perspectives, it won’t be abnormal to see a large section of people nodding at the same thing even though they don’t understand the logic behind the things they’re following, and blindly so.

Those who blindly to an ideology or perspective, often lose arguments and debates, because most blind followers haven’t read their scriptures anyway. And those who slightly have, act overconfident until they face a defeat in a logical discussion.

They believe that their ideology or philosophy is absolutely right or on point. They only see the strengths and not the flaws. However, the more you read and explore, you definitely get a variety of perspectives, then you can’t believe that a particular perspective is the ultimate truth.

But it is not quite simple. When you have read a variety of perspectives, philosophies, and ideologies, you overthink and often don’t find a clarity or peace of mind. It can feel like battling with oneself, seeing several roads and walking on none. Not believing or walking on a particular road might put us in a confusion where we might not reach any destination anyway.

Perspectivism only makes sense if you don’t believe in your ideology blindly but you still follow particular one, but you do have logical reasoning to back it. That’s how you are not blind, and you’re not much confused. This is not perfect, but it is quite better than getting confused to several roads or following one blindly.
