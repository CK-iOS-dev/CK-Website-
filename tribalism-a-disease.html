Tribalism : A disease

Firstly, tribalism means that you prefer the people and things of the tribe you have always lived in, you are too proud of them, you always choose them over others, even if they are better, because you believe that your tribe is the best even though it is not. This was me defining it, it doesn’t mean you are a tribalist person.

There are a few examples which show tribalism :

A French startup launches its AI chatbot, and it is definitely not better than the mainstream AI chatbots. If it expects the French people to prefer it, just because it is French, and if French people use it only because it is French, not better, it is tribalism.

If one sings “India is the better country than all others” this is also tribalism, indeed, India is not. And most nations are not. In fact, very developed nations would struggle to be better than the whole world despite having better position than most nations.

If one says “Apple, Google, Microsoft, NVDIA are big corporations because they are American” that may not be true. Yes, American stock market is a big one. But a stock market is built upon big companies, it is both ways. Those corporations play equal part.

If Apple says “We will build all our products in America, we are patriots”, we would see them become 3-5 times. They don’t see China as a rival, that is why we see iPhones worth less than $1,000.

One should prefer what has merit, what wins because of its own strengths, not because it is from a certain place. This is not only about products, but humanity as well. But we can find such people very easily who still are tribal. But with rising influence of globalism, merit is prioritised, not identity.
