Respect for the elders!! Should you?

We have always been told to respect our elders, we’ve been believing that it is very important for us to respect those who older in age than us. But why? They don’t answer the why clearly. Those who tell us to respect the elders, are elders themselves.

Nobody asks the elders whether they respect us, and likely they respect us far less than we’d ever do. They always consider them superior, experienced, and wiser than we are. Maybe in the past, it was true. Not today. Today, younger ones are capable of being smarter, wiser, and richer than typical adults. Because these things don’t require you to be old but they require you to strategically different.

In this world of Internet and AI, it is not surprising for teens to surpass the knowledge of adults, a 10 years old boy can beat 50 years old man in a debate in today’s era. Yes, with age comes experience, but the experience of such a 10 years old at 50 years of age would be far more ahead, if they just kept themselves consistent.

Neither elders are righteous nor teens are. Yet I have got to say, just because someone is younger comparatively, doesn’t mean he/she should bow to me first, and I would bless them. That doesn’t hold. Many western societies have got ridden of such rigidness. There, you meet if you want to meet. You bow, if you want to bow. You silly shake the hands like equals. In fact, in some families parents have removed n most of the formality, many kids can usually call them by name. Yeah, sounds awkward. But it isn’t.

I am saying that there should be respect in all relationships, but beware, it shouldn’t become submission for the other. It shouldn’t become unequal to another. I say to younger ones, they should not respect me unless I don’t.
